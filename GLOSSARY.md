# Island Adventure RPG

The language of a short, single-hero adventure across a remote island, from a shipwrecked coast to a castle harbor and a way home.

## Language

**Adventure Area**:
One distinct part of the island that the hero explores, such as the woods, mountain, cave, or castle.

**Automap**:
A map of the island route that records terrain as the hero explores it and shows the hero's current location.

**Checkpoint**:
A safe place that records a recovery point during the adventure.

**Experience (XP)**:
Progress earned from defeating monsters and reaching major milestones; enough experience raises the hero's level.

**Level**:
A measure of the hero's growth that grants a stat point.

**Stat Point**:
A point the hero assigns to Health, Attack, or Defense when they grow a level.

**Health**:
The hero's maximum ability to withstand damage.

**Attack**:
The hero's damage-dealing strength.

**Defense**:
The hero's ability to reduce incoming damage.

**Gear**:
Weapons and armor the hero equips to improve combat stats.

**Melee Weapon**:
A close-range weapon, such as the hero's sword.

**Ranged Weapon**:
A weapon that attacks from a distance, such as the hero's bow.

**Ammunition**:
Supplies consumed by ranged attacks.

**Healing Potion**:
A consumable item that restores the hero's Health.

**Guardian**:
The undead defender whose defeat grants access to the castle's forgotten harbor.
