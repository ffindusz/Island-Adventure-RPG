# 36: Esc pauses the game and brings up the start screen

**What to build:** After playing the third build the player asked that Esc pause the game and switch to the start screen with a "paused" message. Today Esc closes the open page, declines the question whether to sail home and otherwise does nothing; the only pauses are the Game core standing still behind a page and the PAUSED notice of a tab nobody is watching. Now an Esc in play with nothing else open (no page, no Stat Point to assign, no question to sail home) puts the play screen away and shows the start screen of the title: the same window with NEW GAME, CONTINUE and CONTROLS, with RESUME on top, a PAUSED message and the cursor on RESUME. The game stays in memory, nothing steps it, and the pause saves nothing. RESUME, or Esc at the top of the paused screen, brings the play screen back with the game exactly as it was. NEW GAME there always asks first, since it loses the game in play and replaces the saved game if there is one; CONTINUE loads the last autosave; CONTROLS shows the list, which Esc or Enter closes without resuming. All of it is the adapter's: nothing in the Game core changes, no command is added, and the saved game's format is as it was.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Spec stories:** 116, 93, 109

- [ ] Nothing in `src/core` changes: no command, no event, no field of the state, and `SNAPSHOT_VERSION` stays 4. - [ ] When Esc pauses is a pure rule of the adapter, with no Phaser, tested with views worked by hand (a new file in `tests/`, named by the implementer). It says to pause when a fresh Esc went down in the frame (not the auto-repeat of a key held down, and not with Ctrl or Meta) and the game was running: the view at the start of the frame and the view at its end are `playing` with no page open, no Stat Point to assign and no question to sail home open (`gameIsWaiting` in `src/adapter/waiting.ts` says it for one view), and the frame's events hold no `hero-defeated`. One test for each reason not to pause: no Esc; an Esc that is a repeat; an Esc with Ctrl, and with Meta; a page open at the start (the Esc closes it); the question to sail home open at the start (the Esc declines it); a Stat Point to assign at the start; a page, a Stat Point or the question open at the end though not at the start (a monster beaten in that frame, which raised the Level; a page key pressed in it); a `hero-defeated` in the frame (the fall is about to be shown); a view that is not `playing` at either end. And the cases that pause: the hero standing, the hero walking, and the text of an area on the screen (the text is not something open; ticket 34 makes the same Esc put it away).
- [ ] Esc keeps every job it has. With a page open it closes the page and does not pause. At the question to sail home it declines it and does not pause: a second Esc then pauses. ## Comments

Added at the player's request after playing the third build. The player wrote "when pressing 'escape' we should pause the game and switch to the 'start' scrrenwith the 'paused' messages". Spec story 116 was written for it.

Decided with the player:

- The paused screen is the title's start screen (NEW GAME, CONTINUE, CONTROLS) with RESUME on top and a PAUSED message.
- Esc on the paused screen resumes.
- Esc pauses only when nothing else is open: with a page open it closes it and at the question to sail home it declines it; a further Esc then pauses.
- The live game stays in memory while paused; pausing does not save; NEW GAME there asks before it replaces anything.

Assumed, not decided with the player:

- That CONTINUE on the paused screen loads the last autosave with no question, and so gives up the progress since it. RESUME is the way back to the game in play, and Continue is a choice to go to the save. A question can be added if it proves too easy to press by mistake.
- That the music of the title plays while paused and the music of the place comes back on RESUME.
- That Esc in the card of the opening, at a Stat Point and in a fall does nothing: it neither pauses nor skips.
- That the PAUSED notice of a tab nobody is watching stays as it is: it is the clock that stops, not a screen, and the focus returns the game by itself.
- That a paused game is not kept across a reload: the title after a reload is the normal one and Continue gives the last autosave.
- That the paused window does not tell of a saved game that cannot be used, because of the room. The implementer may find another way within the room.
- That Ctrl or Meta with Esc is not the game's and does not pause, as with the other keys of the game (`isForTheGame`).

Not decided: a SAVE entry on the paused screen; pausing at the card of the opening or at a Stat Point; a QUIT or "back to the title" entry; a dimmed overlay over the game instead of the start screen. No GLOSSARY.md edit is made here; the name of this pause is a proposal for the player.

For the implementer (the facts are from `integration/island-adventure-rpg` at ebed88e):

- The frame. `PlayScene.update` (`src/adapter/play-scene.ts` from 158) starts with `pollAudioKeys`; the card (`holdForOpening`) and a fall (`showFall`) return early. The frame of the game reads `viewAtStart`, sends `pollWalking` (unless the game waits), the inventory menu, `pollActions` and `departureAnswer`, runs the steps (they stop at a `hero-defeated`), shows the events to the notice and the sprites, reads `view`, sounds the frame and sets `fight = fightAfter(fight, events)`. Then, when the view is the title (the hero has sailed home), it starts the ending or the title (about 219 to 223) and returns; then `autosave(events)` (225); then the branch of a defeat (a `Fall`, return); then the fade of an area change, the notice, the line that says H FOR CONTROLS, `draw`, `lastPlaying` and `playMusicFor(view)`. The hook belongs after the defeat branch with a `return` after the scene change, so that the events of the frame have been sounded, saved and shown before the game is put away.
- `init(start: PlayStart)` (about 111) resets `clock`, `feedback`, `controlsLineTime`, `saveAtOnce`, `openingLines` and `fight`; `create()` builds the layers, calls `useKeyboard('control the hero')` (153) and makes the card from `openingLines`. The play screen is started again for every game of a session (New Game and Continue both do it), so starting it from the core's view is a path that already works.
- `TitleScene` (`src/adapter/title-scene.ts`) has no `init`. `create` reads the saved game (`saves.read()`), opens the menu with `openMenu(saved.status === 'usable')` and makes its keys with `useKeyboard('choose from the title', TITLE_LINE_CORNER)` (54). `update` plays the music of the title, polls the menu and Esc (`pollCancel`, 72), applies `pressMenu`, and starts the play screen (New Game 81, Continue 84) or draws `layoutTitleWindow(menu, saved)`. The scene list is in `src/adapter/index.ts` (44 to 46), with the core given to both scenes.
- `src/adapter/title-menu.ts`: `MENU_ENTRIES` (8), `TitleMenu` (`cursor`, `confirming`, `showingControls`), `TitleKeys` (`step`, `confirm`, `cancel`), `TitleChoice` (33), `openMenu` (39), `selectableEntries` (47), `pressMenu` (57), `soundOfPress` (96). New parameters should default to the unpaused menu, and the `TitleMenu` type may gain a field only in a way that leaves the expectations of `tests/title-menu.test.ts` alone.
- `src/adapter/title-window.ts`: `layoutTitleWindow(menu, saved)` (210), `menuRows` (the question's rows are REPLACE THE SAVED GAME?, ENTER TO REPLACE IT and ESC TO KEEP IT), `messageRows(saved)`, `message(...)` (the first line in `INK.warning`, the rest in `INK.hint`), `ENTRY_LABELS`. The numbers: entry 15 high with no gap between entries, row of text 7, gap 4, padding 12, window 200 wide (about 29 letters of 6 pixels fit across).
- `src/adapter/player-input.ts`: `pollActions` (117) sends the `close-page`s and `cancel-departure` on `justDown(closePageKey)` (about 134) with no check of `repeat`; `pollCancel` (162) is `pressedAgain([closePageKey])`; `pressedAgain` (182) is `justDown && !repeat && counts(press)`; `isForTheGame` (36). `walking` is a record in each `PlayerInput`, all false at the start, that `pollWalking` compares with the keys. `GameScene.useKeyboard` (`game-scene.ts` 42 to 48) builds a new `PlayerInput` for each start of a scene, and Phaser makes new keys for it.
- `src/core/game.ts` (34 to 93): `new-game` (35) and `continue` (42) are accepted in every phase, replace the state and clear the held directions; `move` and `stop` (46 to 57) change the held directions and the facing; `isPaused` (`src/core/pause.ts`) holds back attack, interact, switch-weapon and the step, not the walk. There is no pause command in the core, and the title is reached only by `confirm-departure`.
- The two ways to keep the game while the title shows. (a) Start the title and start the play screen again on RESUME, with the data of a resume: the play screen is made afresh from the core's view, as a Continue makes it. What it costs: the sprites' animations start over, and the few things the adapter holds that the view does not (the fight, the line's time) are carried in the data. (b) Keep the play screen alive (`sleep` and `wake`, or launch the title over it and pause the play screen): nothing is lost, but Phaser's keyboard plugin and its keys live on, so a key let go or pressed while the play screen sleeps leaves `isDown` and a pending `JustDown` stale, and the `walking` record of `PlayerInput` says a key is held that is not (with D held at the pause, a D still held after the resume sends no `move`); and NEW GAME and CONTINUE would have to stop a play screen that sleeps, while the title expects to be the only scene it starts from. The implementer chooses and the Comments say which and why; (a) is the one that uses a path that already works.
- The gotchas, once more: the stale data of a start with none; the first auto-repeat of a held Esc that a new scene's key takes for a press, which is why a repeat does not count as a pause or a resume (it would flicker: pause, resume, pause); `scene.start` inside `update` is queued, so the code after it still runs, and the hook returns; the Esc press is consumed by `JustDown` in `pollActions`; the order of the four stops; and that every Esc works after ten pauses and resumes, in both scenes.

- Tickets 34 and 36 both touch `src/adapter/play-scene.ts` and `src/adapter/player-input.ts`, the README and the checklist (ticket 34 makes any key put away the text of an area): whichever merges second takes the other's lines. When both are in, one Esc press puts the text away and pauses (the dismissal goes first, so that the text is gone when the player resumes): the reviewer of the second checks that. Ticket 35 touches the README and the checklist elsewhere (the Checkpoint), the core, the tests of the balance and `src/adapter/notice.ts`, and none of the files of the input or of the play screen.
