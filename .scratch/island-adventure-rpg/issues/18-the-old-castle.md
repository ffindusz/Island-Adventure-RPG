# 18: The old castle

**What to build:** Turn the stub into the real old castle, up to the end of its route. The undead hold its courtyards and halls, supplies and an armor upgrade prepare the hero for the final fight, and the route ends at a plain wall where the next ticket adds the Guardian's arena. The castle is the fifth and last Adventure Area; its forgotten harbor is part of the same area and arrives in the next two tickets. Art stays placeholder until the art tickets.

**Blocked by:** 17: The cave

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Spec stories:** 9-11, 15, 50, 69

- [x] The castle has an entrance Checkpoint and an exit back to the cave. The layout covers courtyards and halls with at least one optional side path.
- [x] Castle undead are data variants of existing behaviors, including a tougher variant.
- [x] An armor upgrade, plus Ammunition caches and Healing Potions, are placed so the hero can prepare for the final fight.
- [x] The area has one milestone and short area text.
- [x] The route ends at a plain wall that ticket 19 replaces with the way to the Guardian's arena.
- [x] Experience is tuned (data only) so the hero reaches about Level 6 by the end of the route on the intended route, without grinding.
- [x] Content validation passes, including that the main route is reachable without puzzles, and that the five Adventure Areas connect in order: coast, woods, mountain, cave, castle.
- [x] Tests at the Game core's interface load the shipped castle content and confirm that validation passes, the five Adventure Areas connect in order, the main route is walkable from the entrance to the end of the route without any puzzle, and the Experience reachable on the intended route meets the Level 6 target.
- [ ] Reviewer play check (manual, in a browser): arrive from the cave, clear the castle, collect the upgrade, and reach the end of the route.

## Comments

### What was built

- The old castle, `src/content/areas/castle.ts`: 64 by 30 tiles, more than three screens wide and two high (the screen is 20 by 12). The stub's ids stay: the area `castle`, the entrance `from-cave` at (1, 6) with `castle-checkpoint` on its tile, the only Checkpoint, and the exit `to-cave` (west edge, rows 6 and 7, to the cave's `from-castle`). Rows 0 to 3 are rock, so the corner the HUD covers is solid. The area text is one line: "The old castle waits in silence."
- The route is 86 steps long (the straight walk from the way in to its end is 66) and runs through courtyards and halls in turn, each joined to the next by a gateway two tiles wide: the outer yard (grass; its ruined cross wall has a gap of four tiles), the gatehouse passage, the long hall (sand, four pillars), the inner gateway, the inner courtyard (grass, a fountain and four pillars), the door of the great hall, the great hall (sand, four pillars), the great gateway and the last hall (sand, 5 by 7). It ends at (62, 11) against the plain east wall (column 63, solid in every row), on which nothing is drawn or placed.
- Three places lie off the route: the armory, a small alcove of the long hall with the armor chest two steps off the route; the watch tower, a room up a stair from the north side of the long hall; and the crypt, a room with two tombs down a stair from the south side of the inner courtyard. The tower and the crypt are each a good walk from the route and end in a chest of Healing Potions, a chest of arrows and an undead that the route never wakes.
- Undead, data only in `monsters.ts`: four types of the castle's own, variants of the three behaviors: `castle-skeleton` (a chaser, 4 Health, hits for 6), `castle-ghoul` (a charger, 8 Health, hits for 9, a lane of 8 tiles), `castle-wraith` (shoots and keeps its distance, 4 Health, a shot of 6, may drop 3 arrows) and the tougher `castle-knight` (a chaser, 20 Health, hits for 10, 1.7 tiles a second against the hero's 5; may drop a Healing Potion). Ten are placed. On the route, in order: two skeletons in the outer yard, a skeleton and a wraith in the long hall, a skeleton, the ghoul and a wraith in the inner courtyard, and the knight in the great hall. A wraith keeps the watch tower and a ghoul the crypt.
- Finds: the Knight Mail (armor, +3) in `castle-armor-chest`; three caches of 6 arrows (`castle-arrow-cache-1` to `-3`) and three Healing Potions on the ground (`castle-potion-1` to `-3`), a step or two off the route, the last of each past the knight; in the watch tower 2 potions and 8 arrows, in the crypt 3 potions and 8 arrows; and the milestone `great-gateway` (3 Experience) in the great gateway, which every way on takes.
- Adapter, placeholder only: four lines in `MONSTER_COLOURS` (`art.ts`: bone white, pale violet, dark crimson, royal purple) and the Knight Mail's icon, drawn in code (`KNIGHT_ROWS` in `drawn-icons.ts`, its `ARTWORK` line, its mention in `docs/assets/licences.md`). The castle is not in `FINAL_ART_AREAS`: it keeps its flat colours. Tests: `tests/the-castle.test.ts` (40, new). README: the castle, its undead, the Knight Mail, the caches and the potions, the new test file.

### Decisions and defaults

- **The tougher variant is a chaser, the knight** (the spec's default: a tougher variant of an ordinary monster; the one Guardian is ticket 19's). 20 Health against the cave bear's 14 and the mountain veteran's 12, a hit of 10 against 9 and 8, 1.7 tiles a second against the bear's 1.8; it notices the hero from 4 tiles, winds up for 0.9 s and rests for 1.3 s. Tests pin that it has more than twice the Health of any other undead, more Health and a harder hit than any creature of the earlier areas, and a hit that cannot take the whole Health of a rested hero. The great hall lies between the inner courtyard and the last hall, so every way through meets the knight (a test). The numbers leave the Guardian headroom. Each type's comment says how many hits it takes and what it does after Defense. Measured in the smoke against a Level 5 hero (Health 12, Attack 4, Defense 4: one Stat Point in Health, two in Attack, one in Defense, Steel Sword, Plate Armor, Long Bow): the ghoul's one blow cost 5 Health and the knight's 6; both fights were won, and the Healing Potion the knight dropped was picked up.
- **The undead differ from the cave's creatures**: skeletons chase in numbers (four on the route, quicker than any earlier chaser except the bat), the wraith walks and keeps its distance where the cave's eye stood still, and the ghoul hits harder (9) and has more Health (8) than the cave's lizard (7 and 6). Skeletons and wraiths are frail (4 Health), so the castle asks the hero to watch lanes and shots before the one hard fight.
- **Experience, data only** (the thresholds are as they were): skeleton 2, ghoul 5, wraith 3, knight 7, `great-gateway` 3. The route gives 29: eight undead (four skeletons 8, two wraiths 6, the ghoul 5, the knight 7: 26) and the milestone (3). The hero comes in with 75 (the first four areas on their routes), so a hero who fights what notices the route has 104 at the end of it: Level 6 (100) with 4 to spare, and the core caps Experience at 100. The knight is not optional for it: with the knight kept off the route (a mutant) it is 97. The tower's wraith (3) and the crypt's ghoul (5) lie on the side paths and are on top of that. One test pins it over the shipped content, from the coast to the end of the castle's route. **Guesses for ticket 26 to balance:** the four creature values, the milestone's 3, the caches' 6, the side paths' stocks (8 arrows and 2 potions in the tower, 8 and 3 in the crypt), the potions' 1, the drop chances, the Knight Mail's +3.
- **The armor is the Knight Mail, +3** (the Plate Armor's +2 and one more), in `castle-armor-chest` at (19, 12) in the armory: 2 steps off the route, which passes it at step 27, long before the knight is first met (step 65; a test says the chest comes first), so a hero who explores meets the knight better armored and one who does not can still win it with potions. A test pins that it beats every armor the hero begins with or finds in the five areas; another walks the hero to the chest, opens it, wears it and sees Defense rise by 3. **The name:** it was to be the "Knight's Armor"; the Inventory page cuts a name after 11 letters (`fitted()` in `overlay.ts`: 144 pixels for icon, name, bonus and tick, 6 a letter) and the smoke showed "KNIGHT'S AR", so it is the Knight Mail (id `knight-mail`, icon `gear-knight-mail`), and a test keeps the castle's armor to 11 letters. Its icon is drawn in code like the other armors (a dark iron harness with a red stripe), told apart from the Basic Armor, the Chain Mail and the Plate Armor in the inventory page and in the notice of finding it (seen in both). The HUD shows no armor, so it is not there.
- **Supplies for the final fight** lie on the route: three caches of 6 arrows (18) and three Healing Potions, a step or two aside, the last cache (55, 12) and the last potion (55, 4) in the great hall past the knight (tests), so the hero can fill up after the hard fight. The side paths add 16 arrows and 5 potions for a hero who walks them. Nothing is in the last hall.
- **The last hall is empty and quiet**: no monster, chest, pickup, milestone, Checkpoint, entrance or exit is placed in it, and no monster notices a hero there, with a tile to spare (a test; the knight, the nearest, stands 8 tiles from the hall's west edge with a notice of 4), which is the shipped rule that a Checkpoint there must pass. It has a block of open ground 4 tiles square at least, for a Checkpoint and for the hero to stand away from the wall. One constant in the test, `END_OF_ROUTE` (62, 11), names where the route ends.
- **One Checkpoint, at the way in.** The longest way back to it is 89 steps (about 18 s at the hero's 5 tiles a second), from the far corners of the last hall; from the end of the route it is 86, and the knight stands 65 steps from it. A defeat at the knight sends the hero that far back: see the suggestions.
- **No new terrain kind**: rock, sand and grass only. The yards are grass and the halls sand; the walls, the pillars, the fountain, the tombs and the ruined cross wall are rock tiles. There is nothing for `TERRAIN_COLOURS`; ticket 25 draws the castle from the same three kinds (see the suggestions).
- **Passages are at least two tiles wide wherever a charger can notice the hero** (a test), so the ghoul's lane can be stepped out of.
- **Monsters notice by distance, through rock** (the shipped rule, as in the cave). The hall wraith stands at (22, 18) and not at (25, 18): from there its notice of 6 would reach a hero at the crypt's chests. Tests keep the route out of the reach of the two side-path guards and name the guards of each side path's chest that the route meets (none but the wraith and the ghoul placed there).
- **Nothing to find in the bottom three rows** (rows 27 to 29, all rock), where the notice is drawn over the hero: a test over every chest and pickup. The way in and the way back are in rows 6 and 7.
- **`from-cave` stays the castle's first and only entrance** (the saved-game walks start a hero at the first one).
- No rule and no part of the Game core changed; `SNAPSHOT_VERSION` stays 2 (Gear and chests were saved already). `saved-game-walks.test.ts` is untouched: the castle's case takes 17 ms, far from `WALK_TIMEOUT_MS` (30 s).
- The Automap fits five areas: the coast, the woods and the mountain on its top row, the castle (the widest, 64 by 30, highlighted) and the cave below. `automap-layout.ts` is untouched.
- The ticket and the spec do not disagree on anything this ticket touches; the spec's defaults were used (a tougher variant for the demanding fight, no Guardian here).

### How to run it

- `npm.cmd run dev`, open the URL, Enter on the title, Enter on the opening card. Walk the coast, the woods, the mountain and the cave, and leave the cave by its exit forward (east end) into the castle. The way in is the outer yard: through the gap of the cross wall, south-east into the gatehouse passage, east along the long hall, through the inner gateway into the inner courtyard, up its east side to the door of the great hall, through the great hall (the knight) and the great gateway (the milestone) into the last hall: the route ends at the east wall. For the armor, stand at (19, 13) in the armory off the long hall, face up and press E on the chest; I opens the inventory page, Down to the Knight Mail's row and Enter wears it. The tower's stair is at columns 24 and 25, up from the north side of the long hall; the crypt's at columns 34 and 35, down from the south side of the inner courtyard. M shows the Automap with five areas.
- `?debug` exposes `window.gameCore`. To see a place without walking to it, take `gameCore.snapshot()`, patch `state.areaId` (`'castle'`), `state.hero.position` (a tile's centre: column + 0.5, row + 0.5) and, past 10 Experience, `statPoints` to match the Level, then `gameCore.send({ type: 'continue', snapshot })`.
- `npx.cmd vitest run tests/the-castle.test.ts tests/shipped-areas.test.ts tests/saved-game-walks.test.ts` runs the tests of this ticket; `npm.cmd run check` is the gate.
