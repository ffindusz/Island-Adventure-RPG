### Decisions and defaults

- **The tougher variant is a chaser, the bear** (14 Health against 6 at most for the others, a hit of 9 against the lizard's 7), the spec's default (a tougher variant of an ordinary monster, no extra Guardian). The demanding fight asks for Health, Defense and the bow, not a new mechanic. Each type's comment says how many hits it takes and what it does. Measured in the smoke against a Level 4 hero with one Stat Point in each of Health, Attack and Defense and the Chain Mail (Defense 3): the lizard costs 4 Health a hit, the bear 6; the bear takes 5 hits of the Steel Sword (4 of the Katana).
- **The Katana is a melee weapon, +2** (the Steel Sword adds 1). The sword is the hero's main attack and arrows run out, so the upgrade goes where it is used all the time. It ties the Long Bow's +2 but sits in the other slot. A test pins that it beats every melee Gear the hero begins with or finds on the coast, in the woods and on the mountain. Its icon is the pack's `Items\Weapons\Katana\Sprite.png`.
- **A new sword needs nothing beyond its icon** (`gear-<id>.png`, the `ARTWORK` line, the licences line). The inventory page and the notice of finding it draw that icon (seen in both). The HUD draws one weapon picture per slot (`WEAPON_ICONS` in `hud.ts`: `sword-held` for every sword) and the hero's hand holds the same shared sword (`hero-sprite.ts`), so neither shows the Katana's own picture: a pixel comparison of the HUD's weapon slot with the Steel Sword and with the Katana found them identical.
- **Experience, data only** (the thresholds are as they were): bat 2, lizard 4, eye 3, bear 6, `deep-shaft` 3. The trail gives 20 (two bats, the lizard, the eye, the bear, the shaft), so a hero who fights what notices the trail leaves the cave with 75 (55 on arrival): Level 5 (70) with 5 to spare. The grotto's lizard is 4 more, so a hero who clears the coast, the woods, the mountain and the cave has 92, under Level 6 (100): nobody has to grind. Two tests pin both ends. **Guesses for ticket 26 to balance:** the four creature values, the shaft's 3, the caches' 6 and the grotto's 8, the potions' 1 and 3, the Katana's +2.
- **One Checkpoint, at the way in.** The longest way back to it is 115 steps, from the exit forward at the east end (about 23 s at the hero's 5 tiles a second); the bear stands 107 steps from it. A defeat at the bear sends the hero that far back: see the suggestions.
- **No new terrain kind**: rock, sand and grass only, so there is nothing for ticket 24 to add to `TERRAIN_COLOURS`.
- **Passages the hero must use are at least two tiles wide wherever a charger can notice the hero** (a test), so the lizard can be stepped around. The only passage one tile wide is the gate, on the optional side path.
- **The gate is one tile wide, and a hero 0.75 wide must be within an eighth of a tile of its centre line.** The core has no sliding and no corner assist, so a hero can bump the wall beside the gate. The sign stands at (18, 18) so that a hero who keeps to the wall below the gate and slides along it is stopped in line with the gate: the sign is a guide rail as well as a clue. A test walks that route with the shipped hero's real step (5 tiles a second, 12 steps a tile: the fixtures' `STEPS_PER_TILE` of 8 is the arena hero's) and the smoke walked it with real keys. If the playtest finds it fiddly, widen the gate to two tiles with a second gate on the same lever; the pocket's tests that speak of the one gate would follow.
- **Nothing to find in the bottom three rows**, as in the woods and the mountain: the notice is drawn over them. A test keeps every chest, pickup, sign, lever and gate above them; the entrance `from-castle` and the exit forward are in rows 9 and 10.
- **`from-mountain` stays the first entrance of the cave** (the saved-game walks start a hero at the first one); `from-castle` is the second.
- No rule and no part of the Game core changed; `SNAPSHOT_VERSION` stays 2 (the lever's and the gate's state was kept already). `saved-game-walks.test.ts` is untouched: alone, the cave's case takes 11 ms and the castle's 11 ms, the two seeded walks 2.0 s and 2.2 s (about 2.6 s beside the other files), far from `WALK_TIMEOUT_MS` (30 s).
- The Automap fits five areas: the coast, the woods and the mountain on its top row, the castle and the cave below (seen in the screenshots, with the cave highlighted).
