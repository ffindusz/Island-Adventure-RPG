# 31: The hero slides round corners into narrow passages

**What to build:** The player can walk into a passage one tile wide without lining the hero up to the pixel. Today the hero's body is three quarters of a tile wide, so a passage one tile wide leaves it two pixels of room on each side and the Game core has no corner slide: a hero a few pixels off the middle of the crevice in the coast's bay, or of the cabin's door, is stopped flush against the corner at every try. Now, when a step of the hero is stopped by the corner of something solid that does not move, and the hero's middle is already over the gap beside it, the hero slides sideways the little that is needed to clear the corner, at walking speed, and then goes on in the direction it was walking. The help is the hero's alone: monsters move as before, and nothing slides into anything solid.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Spec stories:** 21, 22, 23, 111

- [ ] A hero that stands against the mouth of a passage one tile wide, with its middle anywhere over the gap, holds the move key and walks in. Tested at the Game core's interface on the real content and the real hero, for the two passages the player named: the crevice in the south wall of the coast's bay (column 25, row 13, entered walking down) and the cabin's door in the woods (column 5, row 22, shut at first and then opened with a snapshot or with the key, entered walking down). The hero's middle starts at every offset from the middle of the gap from -0.5 to +0.5 of a tile, in steps of 1/16 or finer and with both ends included (a start where the hero's body does not fit is skipped), and within one second of steps it is past the tile of the gap (its middle is in row 14 of the coast, and in row 23 of the woods).
- [ ] The numbers of the slide are worked out by hand and pinned. With the hero flush above the crevice (middle at y = 12.625, so that its body, three quarters of a tile, touches row 13) and its middle 0.2 tile right of the gap's middle (x = 25.7), its body overlaps the rock of column 26 by 0.075 tile: after one step of walking down it has slid to x = 25.625 and is still at y = 12.625, and after the next it is at y = 12.7083 (a step is 5 tiles a second in 60 steps a second, 0.0833 tile). At the far end of the window (middle at x = 26.0, 0.5 right of the middle of the gap) it needs 0.375 tile and takes five steps to slide, four of 0.0833 and one of 0.0417, to x = 25.625, and walks down from the sixth step. A hero whose middle is a sixteenth beyond that (x = 26.0625, over the rock) is not helped: it stays at x = 26.0625, y = 12.625 however long the key is held. If you split a step differently from the numbers above, work your own out by hand and say so in the comments; no step ever moves the hero farther than a walking step.
- [ ] A hero whose middle is over the wall, the chest, the lever, the sign, the shut door or the shut gate is not helped: it stops flush against it as before, and a hero that walks head-on into a chest, a lever, a sign, a shut door or a shut gate stays flush in front of it, so that `interact` reaches it as it did (each kind is walked up to and used in a test). A slide is never into anything solid: the sideways step stops flush against rock and water, against a chest, lever, sign, shut door and shut gate, and against a living monster (a monster that stands in the way of the slide stops it, and the hero is left flush where it was), and no step ends with the hero overlapping any of them.
- [ ] Facing does not change in a slide: a hero walking down is still facing down after it, and the position is all that the view tells of the slide (no new field, no new event).
- [ ] Only the hero is helped. `moveBody`, which the monsters share, is left as it is, and a test shows that a chasing monster whose body clips the corner of a passage one tile wide is stopped as before (it does not slide), with the monsters' own tests and numbers unchanged.
- [ ] A hero that walks straight in, with its middle within a sixteenth of a tile of the middle of the gap, and a hero that walks in the open, move exactly as before, step by step (a test compares the positions of a walk down the middle of the crevice and a walk across open ground with the positions worked out by hand, which nothing slides in).
- [ ] Every passage one tile wide that the content ships is walkable from anywhere over it. A passage that is shut at first by a door or a gate is tried with it open.

## Comments

Added at the player's request after playing it ("it is very hard to navigate into the narrow passages (eg: house with the door, 'cave' at the bottom of the start screen) - it seems that the step size does not align with the passages (its always slightly 'off')"). Spec story 111, the Movement bullet and the Testing Decisions of the spec were written for it.

Diagnosed, not assumed. The step size is not the cause: the hero's body is 0.75 tile (12 pixels) and the passages are 1 tile (16 pixels), so the hero has 0.125 tile (2 pixels) of room on each side, whatever the step, and `walkHero` (`src/core/movement.ts`) stops it flush against the first corner it meets with no slide at all. A sweep over the five areas of the real content found 98 tile and direction pairs that are passages one tile wide, 82 of them passable only by a hero whose middle is within 0.125 tile of the middle of the gap; of the 2,743 starting offsets tried, 1,665 (61 percent) got stuck. The named places are the crevice in the coast's bay (column 25, row 13) and the cabin's door (column 5, row 22). A prototype of a corner slide for the hero alone got every one of them through (none stuck) and left 3,285 of 3,286 tests green: the one that failed is the coverage assertion of `saved-game-walks` (above).

Decided with the player: a slide that helps the hero only, with no change to the monsters or to any balance. The player turned down two other ways: a smaller hero (it doubles the window to 0.25 tile but changes how combat and the monsters' reach feel) and wider passages (they stop being narrow and lose the secret crevice).

Assumed, not decided with the player: that the slide goes round the corners of chests, levers, signs and shut doors as it does round rock (they are all solid things that do not move, and a hero should not catch on one more than on another), and that "the middle is over the gap" is the limit (the hero's middle within the width of the gap, which is the smallest limit that makes every passage of the shipped content walkable from anywhere over it). Not decided: any sign of the slide in the picture or the sound. There is none: the hero keeps its walking animation and its facing, and the player can say if it should look different.

For the implementer: `walkHero` and `moveBody` are in `src/core/movement.ts`; `src/core/geometry.ts` has `boxAround`, `overlaps`, `EPSILON` and `travelledBetween`, and `src/core/obstacles.ts` has `stopsWalkers`, which says which interactables stop a walker (a shut door or gate does, an open one does not). The hero is 0.75 tile (`src/content/hero.ts`), 5 tiles a second, so `speedPerStep` is 0.0833 tile (12 steps a tile). The fixture arena of `tests/fixtures.ts` has a hero of 0.5 tile and a step of 0.125 tile, which is not the real hero: the real passages are tested with the real content (`tests/profile-heroes.ts` has `gameWithHero` and `QUIET`, which set a real hero down in a real area, and takes a snapshot hook to open a door: `save.state.areas['woods'].interactables['cabin-door'].open = true`).

A design that was tried and worked, as a sketch and not the answer (tests first, and your own clean implementation): after `moveBody` gives where the step ends, if more than `EPSILON` of the step is unspent, find the things in the way that do not move (solid tiles, and the interactables that stop walkers, never the monsters); for each of the two sideways directions work out the shift that clears every one of them, and take the smaller; slide only if that shift is at most half the hero's size (plus `EPSILON`, so that the end of the window is in); move the hero sideways by the smaller of the shift and the unspent distance with `moveBody` against everything, living monsters too, so that the slide can't enter what is solid; leave the facing alone. A step that slides moves the hero sideways only, and the next one goes on in the direction it was walking. A shut door ahead is a blocker as wide as the gap, so a hero in front of it has no shift that clears it and stays flush. `moveBody` is shared with the monsters: leave it as it is.

`tests/saved-game-walks.test.ts`: the gauntlet is the test's own fixture arena (`arena({ layout: FIELD_LAYOUT, ... })`), the hero at (6.5, 3.5) in a pocket between `armor-chest` (5,3), `bow-chest` (7,3, which holds the fixture's `short-bow`) and `arrow-cache` (6,4); `walkAtRandom(content, seed, rounds, walk)` plays `commandAtRandom` for 1 to 8 steps a round and saves and parses the snapshot every round. The `short-bow` that this arena's chest holds is the fixture's own: it has nothing to do with the shipped content (ticket 32 removes the shipped Short Bow and leaves this fixture alone). Vitest here has no `basic` reporter and keeps the output of a passing test: write what you want to see to a file.
