# 31: The hero slides round corners into narrow passages

**What to build:** The player can walk into a passage one tile wide without lining the hero up to the pixel. Today the hero's body is three quarters of a tile wide, so a passage one tile wide leaves it two pixels of room on each side and the Game core has no corner slide: a hero a few pixels off the middle of the crevice in the coast's bay, or of the cabin's door, is stopped flush against the corner at every try. Now, when a step of the hero is stopped by the corner of something solid that does not move, and the hero's middle is already over the gap beside it, the hero slides sideways the little that is needed to clear the corner, at walking speed, and then goes on in the direction it was walking. The help is the hero's alone: monsters move as before, and nothing slides into anything solid.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** resolved

**Spec stories:** 21, 22, 23, 111

- [x] A hero that stands against the mouth of a passage one tile wide, with its middle anywhere over the gap, holds the move key and walks in. Tested at the Game core's interface on the real content and the real hero, for the two passages the player named: the crevice in the south wall of the coast's bay (column 25, row 13, entered walking down) and the cabin's door in the woods (column 5, row 22, shut at first and then opened with a snapshot or with the key, entered walking down). The hero's middle starts at every offset from the middle of the gap from -0.5 to +0.5 of a tile, in steps of 1/16 or finer and with both ends included (a start where the hero's body does not fit is skipped), and within one second of steps it is past the tile of the gap (its middle is in row 14 of the coast, and in row 23 of the woods).
- [x] The numbers of the slide are worked out by hand and pinned. With the hero flush above the crevice (middle at y = 12.625, so that its body, three quarters of a tile, touches row 13) and its middle 0.2 tile right of the gap's middle (x = 25.7), its body overlaps the rock of column 26 by 0.075 tile: after one step of walking down it has slid to x = 25.625 and is still at y = 12.625, and after the next it is at y = 12.7083 (a step is 5 tiles a second in 60 steps a second, 0.0833 tile). At the far end of the window (middle at x = 26.0, 0.5 right of the middle of the gap) it needs 0.375 tile and takes five steps to slide, four of 0.0833 and one of 0.0417, to x = 25.625, and walks down from the sixth step. A hero whose middle is a sixteenth beyond that (x = 26.0625, over the rock) is not helped: it stays at x = 26.0625, y = 12.625 however long the key is held. If you split a step differently from the numbers above, work your own out by hand and say so in the comments; no step ever moves the hero farther than a walking step.
- [x] A hero whose middle is over the wall, the chest, the lever, the sign, the shut door or the shut gate is not helped: it stops flush against it as before, and a hero that walks head-on into a chest, a lever, a sign, a shut door or a shut gate stays flush in front of it, so that `interact` reaches it as it did (each kind is walked up to and used in a test). A slide is never into anything solid: the sideways step stops flush against rock and water, against a chest, lever, sign, shut door and shut gate, and against a living monster (a monster that stands in the way of the slide stops it, and the hero is left flush where it was), and no step ends with the hero overlapping any of them.

## Comments

Added at the player's request after playing it ("it is very hard to navigate into the narrow passages (eg: house with the door, 'cave' at the bottom of the start screen) - it seems that the step size does not align with the passages (its always slightly 'off')"). Spec story 111, the Movement bullet and the Testing Decisions of the spec were written for it.

Diagnosed, not assumed. The step size is not the cause: the hero's body is 0.75 tile (12 pixels) and the passages are 1 tile (16 pixels), so the hero has 0.125 tile (2 pixels) of room on each side, whatever the step, and `walkHero` (`src/core/movement.ts`) stops it flush against the first corner it meets with no slide at all. A sweep over the five areas of the real content found 98 tile and direction pairs that are passages one tile wide, 82 of them passable only by a hero whose middle is within 0.125 tile of the middle of the gap; of the 2,743 starting offsets tried, 1,665 (61 percent) got stuck. The named places are the crevice in the coast's bay (column 25, row 13) and the cabin's door (column 5, row 22). A prototype of a corner slide for the hero alone got every one of them through (none stuck) and left 3,285 of 3,286 tests green: the one that failed is the coverage assertion of `saved-game-walks` (above).

Decided with the player: a slide that helps the hero only, with no change to the monsters or to any balance. The player turned down two other ways: a smaller hero (it doubles the window to 0.25 tile but changes how combat and the monsters' reach feel) and wider passages (they stop being narrow and lose the secret crevice).

Assumed, not decided with the player: that the slide goes round the corners of chests, levers, signs and shut doors as it does round rock (they are all solid things that do not move, and a hero should not catch on one more than on another), and that "the middle is over the gap" is the limit (the hero's middle within the width of the gap, which is the smallest limit that makes every passage of the shipped content walkable from anywhere over it). Not decided: any sign of the slide in the picture or the sound. There is none: the hero keeps its walking animation and its facing, and the player can say if it should look different.

For the implementer: `walkHero` and `moveBody` are in `src/core/movement.ts`; `src/core/geometry.ts` has `boxAround`, `overlaps`, `EPSILON` and `travelledBetween`, and `src/core/obstacles.ts` has `stopsWalkers`, which says which interactables stop a walker (a shut door or gate does, an open one does not). The hero is 0.75 tile (`src/content/hero.ts`), 5 tiles a second, so `speedPerStep` is 0.0833 tile (12 steps a tile). The fixture arena of `tests/fixtures.ts` has a hero of 0.5 tile and a step of 0.125 tile, which is not the real hero: the real passages are tested with the real content (`tests/profile-heroes.ts` has `gameWithHero` and `QUIET`, which set a real hero down in a real area, and takes a snapshot hook to open a door: `save.state.areas['woods'].interactables['cabin-door'].open = true`).

A design that was tried and worked, as a sketch and not the answer (tests first, and your own clean implementation): after `moveBody` gives where the step ends, if more than `EPSILON` of the step is unspent, find the things in the way that do not move (solid tiles, and the interactables that stop walkers, never the monsters); for each of the two sideways directions work out the shift that clears every one of them, and take the smaller; slide only if that shift is at most half the hero's size (plus `EPSILON`, so that the end of the window is in); move the hero sideways by the smaller of the shift and the unspent distance with `moveBody` against everything, living monsters too, so that the slide can't enter what is solid; leave the facing alone. A step that slides moves the hero sideways only, and the next one goes on in the direction it was walking. A shut door ahead is a blocker as wide as the gap, so a hero in front of it has no shift that clears it and stays flush. `moveBody` is shared with the monsters: leave it as it is.

`tests/saved-game-walks.test.ts`: the gauntlet is the test's own fixture arena (`arena({ layout: FIELD_LAYOUT, ... })`), the hero at (6.5, 3.5) in a pocket between `armor-chest` (5,3), `bow-chest` (7,3, which holds the fixture's `short-bow`) and `arrow-cache` (6,4); `walkAtRandom(content, seed, rounds, walk)` plays `commandAtRandom` for 1 to 8 steps a round and saves and parses the snapshot every round. The `short-bow` that this arena's chest holds is the fixture's own: it has nothing to do with the shipped content (ticket 32 removes the shipped Short Bow and leaves this fixture alone). Vitest here has no `basic` reporter and keeps the output of a passing test: write what you want to see to a file.

### What was built

- **The slide.** `walkHero` (`src/core/movement.ts`) walks the hero as it did, with `moveBody`, against the living monsters and the interactables that stop a walker. When more than `EPSILON` of the step is left unspent, `slideRoundCorner` takes what stood in the way and does not move (the solid tiles and the boxes of those interactables, never the monsters), works out for each of the two sideways directions the shift that clears all of it, takes the smaller, and slides only if that shift is at most half the hero's width (`SLIDE_LIMIT`, plus `EPSILON`, so that the end of the window is in). The hero moves sideways by the smaller of the shift and what is left of the step, with `moveBody` against everything, living monsters too. Nothing else in `src` changed: `moveBody`, `walkMonster`, the arrows, the facing, the view, the events and `SNAPSHOT_VERSION` (4) are as they were.
- **`tests/corner-slide.test.ts`** (51 tests, at the Game core's interface, with numbers worked out by hand). The crevice of the coast's bay (5). A yard with one thing on a tile, for each of rock, water, a chest, a lever, a sign, a shut door and a shut gate (3 each): the hero slides round it when its middle is over the gap, slides at the very edge of the window and is not helped a sixteenth beyond it, and walks head-on into it and stays flush, so that Interact reaches it as it did (the chest opens, the lever is pulled, the sign is read, the shut door says that it is locked; Interact does not reach a gate, a lever opens it). A monster standing where the slide would go (2), and a hero that walks into a monster (1). A biter at the corner of a wall, that is not helped, and the same biter lined up, that walks in (2). A slide along a rock in each of the four directions and on both sides (8). A step that comes to the corner part of the way through (1). The walks that no corner stops, as they were (8). Seeded walks of 1500 rounds about a room full of things, among them five dummies that stand off the grid of the tiles, where no step ends inside one of them or farther on than a step (3).
- **`tests/passages.test.ts`** (16 tests). `passagesOf` finds the passages one tile wide from the maps and the placed obstacles of each of the five areas alone (open, open behind it, and not open on either side; doors and gates open). ### Decisions and defaults

- **Where the ticket's numbers hold, and where mine begin.** The ticket's numbers are for a hero flush against the corner, where the whole step goes into the slide, and the tests pin them as written: with its middle at x = 25.7 the hero slides 0.075 to x = 25.625 in the first step, still at y = 12.625 (what is left of that step, 0.0083, is not spent), and is at y = 12.7083 after the second; at the far end (x = 26.0) it takes five steps, four of a twelfth and one of half a twelfth, and walks down from the sixth; at x = 26.0625 it does not move however long the key is held. The ticket has no number for a hero that comes to the corner part of the way through a step, so I worked out my own and pinned them: with its middle at (5.175, 3.55), 0.075 above a rock, the first step walks 0.075 and slides the 1/120 that is left, the next two slide a twelfth each, the fourth slides the 0.025 that remains, to x = 5.375, and the fifth walks down. What is left of the step that ends a slide is lost, so no step moves the hero farther than a walking step.
- **The window is inclusive.** A hero whose middle is exactly on the edge of the gap is helped (a shift of half its width, plus `EPSILON`); a sixteenth beyond it, over the thing, is not.
- **Monsters are never corners, and they stop the slide.** A monster moves, so a hero that walks into one is not slid round it. The sideways step is checked against the living monsters: one that stands against that side of the hero leaves it where it was, and one farther off stops it flush when it comes to it.
- **A vessel is a corner too**, since `stopsWalkers` says that it stops a walker as a chest does and it goes through the same code, but no test of its own pins it: the ticket's list does not name it.
- **The scripted shot of the gauntlet.** The opening of every walk in `saved-game-walks.test.ts`: face down and interact (the Ammunition of the cache), face right and interact (the bow of the chest), open the inventory, equip `short-bow`, close it, switch to the bow, face left and attack, wanting `hero-shot` as the only event. The walk saves right after it, with the arrow in the air, and after every round as before. The seeds stay `[1, 2, 3, 4]` at 600 rounds each and no other assertion was touched. The `short-bow` is the arena's own (its chest holds it), not the shipped content's, so ticket 32 leaves it alone.
- **The sweep's world** has every door and gate open and no monster, as the ticket says. The cabin door shut is pinned by place, and a shut door and a shut gate by the yard. The sweep wants `tried` to be more than the number of passages and not a fixed count, so that a passage added to the content is covered without a line in the test, and a sweep that walked nothing fails.
- **Nothing in the picture or the sound** shows the slide: the hero keeps its walking animation and its facing, as the ticket decided.
- **Saves.** The slide has no state, a position in and a position out. `state.ts`, `interface.ts` and `saved-game.ts` are untouched, the snapshot's shape is as it was and `SNAPSHOT_VERSION` stays 4, so a save made before this ticket loads and goes on.
- **Seams.** The tests go through the Game core's interface (ADR 0002 as widened by ADR 0005), a real hero set down by `gameWithHero` and `QUIET`, and nothing is mocked. No ADR is proposed.

A note on the sideways step and what is solid. Only a living monster can show that the sideways step is checked: the slide is the least shift that clears the corner, so it ends in the columns (or rows) that the hero's body already overlapped, and a hero that overlaps nothing solid before the step overlaps nothing solid after it. Rock, water and the interactables sit on the grid of the tiles, so they cannot be in the way of a slide that is allowed, and I found no break that a test of theirs could see. The monsters are not on the grid, and the two monster tests and the seeded walk of seed 2, whose dummies stand off it, are what fail. The seeded walks (no step ends inside anything) are the proof that a slide never ends in anything solid.
