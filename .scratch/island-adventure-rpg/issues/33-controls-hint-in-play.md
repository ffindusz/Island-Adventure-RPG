# 33: The game says that H shows the controls

**What to build:** A player finds the Controls page without looking for it on the title screen. After playing the second build the player chose to keep H as the key of the page and to have the game hint at it in play. Two places say it, and both are the adapter's alone: the card of the opening has one more line under ENTER TO BEGIN, and for a few seconds after play begins (when the player ends the card of a New Game, and when a saved game is continued) a short line on the screen reads H FOR CONTROLS. Both are made from the controls table, so a key that is changed changes the words. The foot of a page does not say it, because while a page is open the key of another page does nothing. The Game core, the saved game and the Controls page itself do not change.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Spec stories:** 112, 109

- [ ] The card of the opening says that H shows the controls: one more line of the hint, under ENTER TO BEGIN and in the same colour, with the key read from the controls table (`pageKeyLabel('controls')`) so that a key that is changed changes the card. The card takes only Enter and holds the game (H does nothing at it), so the line must not promise that H works there: it says what H does in play, such as `IN PLAY, H SHOWS THE CONTROLS` (the wording is the implementer's, and it uses only what the pixel font draws). The card stays whole on the screen and in the middle of it, with as much air above its first row as below its last line, for the opening that the game ships, for the tallest opening the Game core takes (four lines, each wider than the card) and for one short line; and the new line fits the window and is centred in it. The tests of the card (`tests/opening-card.test.ts`) get hand-worked numbers for the new line, and the test of the air is stated again for the last line, not weakened.
- [ ] The ending and the credits are not touched. Their cards share `layoutRows` and `drawCard` with the opening (`src/adapter/ending-pages.ts`) and come out exactly as before: `tests/ending-pages.test.ts`, `tests/the-credits.test.ts`, `tests/audio-line.test.ts` and `tests/shipped-licences.test.ts` pass without an edit. The new line is the opening's alone (for instance an optional second hint that only `layoutCard` passes).
- [ ] A short line says H FOR CONTROLS when play begins. It is on the screen from the first frame in which the game runs after the card (the frame after the player's Enter), or at once for a New Game whose content has no opening, or after Continue. It stays a few seconds, a named constant of about 4 (the time an area's text stays): the implementer chooses between 3 and 5 and the Comments say which. It goes by itself and does not come back in the same game: not after a fall, not in another area, not after a page closes. A game that follows another in the same session (the hero sails home and the player chooses New Game or Continue at the title) shows it again. Nothing is remembered between games: no flag is saved, `SNAPSHOT_VERSION` stays 4, and no save is read or written for it.
- [ ] The time of the line counts only while the game runs. A frame counts when the player is watching (`playerIsWatching` in `src/adapter/play-scene.ts`), the card is gone, no fall is on and the game does not wait (`gameIsWaiting`: a page is open, a Stat Point is to be assigned, or the question whether to sail home is asked). While the game waits the line is off the screen and keeps its time, as the text of an area does, and it is back when the game runs on, with the time it had left. Opening the Controls page ends it for good, since the player has found the page. The frames of the card, of a fall and of a tab nobody watches take nothing from it.
- [ ] The line is clear of everything else on the play screen. A pure function gives its box, as `audioLineBox` does for the audio line, and tests (ADR 0005) pin by hand-worked pixels that the box is whole on the screen and clear of the HUD (`HUD_RIGHT`, `HUD_BOTTOM`), of the text of an area (which starts a few pixels under the HUD) and of both corners of the audio line at its widest text, `VOLUME 10/10`. The notice sits low on the screen, so a place in the top row never meets it; if the line goes low instead, it gives way to a notice and the Comments say how. The place is the implementer's (see the room in the notes below). The line takes no input and holds nothing back: no key is read for it and no step is delayed.
- [ ] It draws over the HUD and the text of an area and under the pages, so that a page that opens covers it, and under the card of the opening: a new entry in `DEPTH` (`src/adapter/depths.ts`) and in the `PLAY_SCREEN` list of `tests/depths.test.ts` in that place, with nothing over the audio line (that test passes as it is).
- [ ] It is the adapter's alone. The Game core has no new command, event, view field, page rule or state, and the saved game is as it was: the tests at the core's interface and of the saved game pass without an edit. The words are made by pure functions of the controls table in `src/adapter/controls.ts`, beside `pageCloseHint`: one for the line (`H FOR CONTROLS`) and one for the card, both reading `pageKeyLabel('controls')`.
- [ ] The foot of a page does not say it. `pageCloseHint` and the foot of every page (the stat page, the inventory, the Automap and the Controls page) are as they were, and `tests/page-hints.test.ts` passes without an edit. A test says that no page's hint contains the words of the line: while a page is open the key of another page does nothing, so a foot that said H FOR CONTROLS would name a key that does not work.
- [ ] The pure rules are tested directly (ADR 0005), by hand-worked numbers: the words (the by-hand literals, and the key read from the table); the time of the line, as a class of its own with no Phaser like the other pure rules of the adapter (on for the first frame, still on one millisecond before its time is spent and gone when it is; the time of a frame in which the game waits or nobody watches takes nothing; it returns after a wait with the time it had left; dismissing ends it for good; a new one starts afresh); the box; the card's layout; and the order of the depths. ## Comments

Added at the player's request after playing it. Asked what to do about the Controls page, which a player would not find in play, the player answered "Keep H and hint at it in play". Spec story 112 and the Menus bullet of the spec were written for it.

Decided with the player: H stays the key of the Controls page, and the game hints at it in play, on the card of the opening and in a short timed line when play begins or continues.

Assumed, not decided with the player:

- That the hint is the adapter's alone and is not on the foot of a page. A page key does nothing while another page is open (the pause rule of ticket 27), so a foot that said H FOR CONTROLS would name a key that does not work on that page. The Controls page keeps its own foot, H OR ESC TO CLOSE.
- The words. The card says what H does in play (the card takes only Enter, so it cannot say that H works at the card); the line reads H FOR CONTROLS, as the choice was put to the player. The player can reword either.
- The time: about 4 seconds, counted in the frames in which the game runs, so a player who opens a page at once does not lose it and a tab nobody watches does not spend it.
- That it shows at every New Game and every Continue and not once ever. Nothing would remember "once" without a flag in the save or a key of its own in the browser's storage, which is more than a hint is worth. If it grows tiresome the player can say.
- That opening the Controls page ends the line for good: the player has found it.
- That the HUD has no permanent mark for H. The player chose a timed line, and the HUD has no room to spare (88 x 46 in the top left corner).

Not decided: whether H should also open the Controls page from the card of the opening. The card holds the game and discards every key but Enter; making H work there would make the page a thing of the card as well, which is another ticket if the player wants it. Where on the screen the line sits is left to the implementer, and the play check asks the player whether it is right.

For the implementer (the facts are from `integration/island-adventure-rpg` at 0f46786):

- The card is in `src/adapter/opening-card.ts`. `OPENING_HINT` is `ENTER TO BEGIN`, made from `confirmKeyLabel()`. `layoutCard(lines)` calls `layoutRows(lines, hint)`, which puts the rows and the one hint (`CardLayout.hint`, a `Placed`) in a window sized round them and centred, so that the air above the first row equals the air below the hint (`tests/opening-card.test.ts` says so). `drawCard` draws the rows in `INK.text` and the hint in `INK.hint`. The ending and the credits (`layoutEnding` in `src/adapter/ending-pages.ts`) call `layoutRows` with their own hints (`CONTINUE_HINT`, `TITLE_HINT`) and their tests read `CardLayout.hint`: give the opening's extra line its own way in and leave the shape that the ending reads alone. The tallest card the Game core takes (four lines, each wider than the card, so eight rows) is 147 high now of the 168 that `CARD_MAX_HEIGHT` allows (the window's padding is 12, `WINDOW_PADDING`); a second hint row 4 pixels under the first (the `ROW_GAP`) makes it 158, and a gap as large as the one before the hint (14) would make it exactly 168, which is the most it may be. The test with the long lines says so.
- The play screen is `src/adapter/play-scene.ts`. `init` runs once for each game of a session and makes anew what the scene keeps (`this.feedback`, `this.clock`): the line's state goes there. `update` returns early for the card (`holdForOpening`, which discards the keys but Enter and ends the card with a fade in of `OPENING_FADE_MS`) and for a fall. `this.banner.draw(gameIsWaiting(view) ? null : view.areaText)` is the model for hiding while the game waits. `letTimePass(frameMs)` is called in the frames of a fall too, so the line's time must not hang on it. The core's clock for the text of the start area also starts at the first frame the game runs, so the text and the line begin together and each stays about 4 seconds (`areaTextMs`, default 4000); the text of the start area runs across the screen under the HUD, so the line must not sit where it runs.
- The model for the line is `src/adapter/audio-line.ts`: the pure `audioLineBox` (hand-worked in `tests/audio-line.test.ts`: `SOUND ON` is 59 x 19 at (257, 4), `VOLUME 10/10` is 83 x 19 at (233, 4)), the frames' time and not a timer, `ChangeGuard` to draw again only when what is shown changes, `UiLayer`, `INK`, and the panel's frame at 0.9 alpha with a margin of 4 and a padding of 6. `H FOR CONTROLS` is 83 pixels of text (14 letters of 6, less the 1 pixel after the last), so the same panel is 95 x 19.
- The room on the play screen: the HUD is (2, 2) to (90, 48) (`HUD_RIGHT`, `HUD_BOTTOM` in `src/adapter/hud.ts`); the text of an area starts at y = 52 (`TOP` in `src/adapter/area-banner.ts`, which is not exported: export what the test needs) and runs from x = 6 to x = 314; the notice is low (10 above the bottom edge, `BOTTOM_MARGIN` in `src/adapter/notice.ts`) and as wide as its text; the audio line is in the top right corner, 4 from each edge, and is 83 wide at the most. The top row between the HUD and the audio line is therefore 143 pixels wide (from 90 to 233) and 48 pixels high (from the top edge to the text of the area), which holds a box of 95 x 19 clear of all of them; the implementer measures it and chooses.
- The key is `pageKeyLabel('controls')` in `src/adapter/controls.ts` (H); `pageCloseHint` builds the feet of the pages from the same table and stays as it is. The pixel font draws 0-9, A-Z, `. , ! ? ' - + / : ( ) % =` and the space; `charactersTheFontLacks` (`tests/drawn-text.ts`) is the check, as in `tests/opening-card.test.ts`.
- `DEPTH` in `src/adapter/depths.ts` holds hud 100, areaBanner 120, notice 150, overlay 200, openingCard 250 and audioLine 400; `tests/depths.test.ts` keeps the order of the `PLAY_SCREEN` list.
