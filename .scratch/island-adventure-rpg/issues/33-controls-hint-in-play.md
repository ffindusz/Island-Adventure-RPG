# 33: The game says that H shows the controls

**What to build:** A player finds the Controls page without looking for it on the title screen. After playing the second build the player chose to keep H as the key of the page and to have the game hint at it in play. Two places say it, and both are the adapter's alone: the card of the opening has one more line under ENTER TO BEGIN, and for a few seconds after play begins (when the player ends the card of a New Game, and when a saved game is continued) a short line on the screen reads H FOR CONTROLS. Both are made from the controls table, so a key that is changed changes the words. The foot of a page does not say it, because while a page is open the key of another page does nothing. The Game core, the saved game and the Controls page itself do not change.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** resolved

**Spec stories:** 112, 109

## Comments

Added at the player's request after playing it. Asked what to do about the Controls page, which a player would not find in play, the player answered "Keep H and hint at it in play". Spec story 112 and the Menus bullet of the spec were written for it.

Decided with the player: H stays the key of the Controls page, and the game hints at it in play, on the card of the opening and in a short timed line when play begins or continues.

Assumed, not decided with the player:

- That the hint is the adapter's alone and is not on the foot of a page. A page key does nothing while another page is open (the pause rule of ticket 27), so a foot that said H FOR CONTROLS would name a key that does not work on that page. The Controls page keeps its own foot, H OR ESC TO CLOSE.
- The words. The card says what H does in play (the card takes only Enter, so it cannot say that H works at the card); the line reads H FOR CONTROLS, as the choice was put to the player. The player can reword either.
- The time: about 4 seconds, counted in the frames in which the game runs, so a player who opens a page at once does not lose it and a tab nobody watches does not spend it.
- That it shows at every New Game and every Continue and not once ever. Nothing would remember "once" without a flag in the save or a key of its own in the browser's storage, which is more than a hint is worth. If it grows tiresome the player can say.
- That opening the Controls page ends the line for good: the player has found it.
- That the HUD has no permanent mark for H. The player chose a timed line, and the HUD has no room to spare (88 x 46 in the top left corner).

Not decided: whether H should also open the Controls page from the card of the opening. The card holds the game and discards every key but Enter; making H work there would make the page a thing of the card as well, which is another ticket if the player wants it. Where on the screen the line sits is left to the implementer, and the play check asks the player whether it is right.
