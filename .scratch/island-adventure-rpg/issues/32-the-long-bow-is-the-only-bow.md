# 32: The Long Bow in the cabin is the only bow

**What to build:** The only bow of the game is the Long Bow in the chest of the cabin in the woods, behind the locked door whose key lies in the north woods. After playing the second build the player chose it over the bow on the wolf. The wolf at the start of the woods stays where it is and carries nothing; the Short Bow is taken out of the game (its Gear, its picture and every word about it); and since no monster hands out Gear any more, the mechanism that let a monster carry a piece of Gear goes too. The Ammunition caches along the trail stay, so that a hero who finds arrows and no bow has a reason to look for one. The main route gives the hero no bow now: ranged play is the side path of the cabin, and the balance tables are measured again for that.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Spec stories:** 113, 110 (superseded by 113), 68, 71, 11, 28, 29, 33, 37, 39, 66

## Comments

Added at the player's request after playing it ("remove the bow from the wolf and lets keep the bow in the treasure in the house - works better!"). Spec story 113, the Gear bullet, the Testing Decisions and Further Notes of the spec were written for it; story 110 and ticket 30 (the bow on the wolf) are superseded.

Decided with the player: "Cabin chest keeps the Long Bow as the only bow: the wolf carries nothing and the Short Bow is removed." The cabin, its door, the brass key in the north woods guarded by `woods-wolf-2`, and the sign by the door stay as they are.

Assumed, not decided with the player:

- That the mechanism of a monster that carries Gear goes with the Short Bow. Nothing in the shipped content would use it, and the spec's story for it is superseded, so it would be a mechanism with no need behind it. If the player wants it kept for later, the fourth criterion shrinks to the Short Bow's part: `tests/carried-gear.test.ts` and the loader's checks stay and the shipped content simply has no carrier (the notice tests of the feedback then stay as they are).
- That the wolf stays where it is, of the same kind: no new monster, no new picture, no change to the Experience of the woods or to any fight of the route.
- That the Long Bow keeps its bonus of 2. It is the only bow, so its step is no longer "+33 to +50 percent over the Short Bow" but "new": nothing is retuned.
- That the arrow caches along the trail stay where they are, with no sign pointing at the cabin: arrows with no bow are the hint. A hero that never goes to the cabin finds them useless; the player can say if that is wrong.
- That the picture of the bow in the hero's hand (`bow-held.png`, the pack's Bow) stays as it is while the icons show the Long Bow (the pack's Bow2). Nothing in the game draws the two side by side.
- That a save that holds the Short Bow is refused, as every earlier change of the content has refused the saves it broke, and not changed to fit. The player's own playtest save may be one: if it holds the Short Bow (the wolf was defeated and the bow taken), it will not load, and a New Game replaces it.

Not decided: whether anything on the trail or in the text of the woods should point at the cabin. Nothing is added.
