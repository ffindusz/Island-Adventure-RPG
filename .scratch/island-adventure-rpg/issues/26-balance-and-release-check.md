# 26: Balance and release check

**What to build:** A final pass that turns the finished pieces into a coherent 45-60 minute adventure that is ready to share. Balance is tuned through content data only, the whole route is validated end to end, the production build is checked on plain static hosting, the asset licence record and credits are completed, and the user gets a short checklist for a full playtest. Any rule change this pass reveals becomes its own new ticket rather than being slipped in here.

**Blocked by:** 21: Audio; 25: Art pass: the castle, the harbor, the Guardian, and the screens

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Spec stories:** 20, 52, 59, 60, 66, 99, 105, 107

- [x] Experience along the intended route gives about five level-ups: around Level 2 at the coast's exit, 3 at the woods', 4 at the mountain's, 5 at the cave's, and Level 6 by the Guardian's arena, never more than the cap of 6. A content test sums the route's Experience (monsters and milestones) against the Level thresholds, and a play-through confirms it feels right with no grinding.
- [x] Most fights are approachable and only a few are demanding (the tougher variants and the Guardian). Weapon and armor upgrades are noticeable steps up, found on or near the route, and the Ammunition and Healing Potions along the route are enough for a player who uses them sensibly, so no one has to grind for supplies.
- [x] A simulation or analytic check at the Game core's interface shows the Guardian can be beaten with the Levels and Gear of the intended route, both by a hero who takes only the main route's Gear and by one who also took the optional upgrades (who finds it easier), and that it is not a pushover for a hero well below the intended Level.
- [ ] A full route's length and encounter count are summed into an estimate of about 45-60 minutes for a first playthrough, with the basis recorded. The user's playtest confirms or corrects it.
- [ ] All balance numbers (damage, Health, Experience thresholds, cooldowns, drop rates) live in content data, and this pass changes data only.
- [ ] A production build, served from a plain static file server with no server component, plays from the title screen to the ending.
- [ ] The asset licence record is complete: every graphic, audio, and font asset in the build has its source, licence, date checked, and required attribution text, and every licence allows public and commercial use. A test fails if a shipped asset has no record. The credits screen shows every required attribution.
- [x] Whole-route content validation passes: five Adventure Areas in order, valid exits in both directions, an entrance Checkpoint in every area and one immediately before the Guardian, exactly one Guardian, the harbor reachable only through it, and no key, lever, or door required on the main route.
- [ ] Regression checks hold: a corrupt or incompatible save is treated as no save without crashing, the game pauses while the browser tab is unfocused, the canvas scales with crisp pixels, and Continue after a Checkpoint reproduces the saved state.
- [ ] Every screen and control, including the audio controls from ticket 21, matches the final interface style. Any leftover is brought in line (presentation only).
- [ ] A short playtest checklist for the user is kept with the project documents. It covers the opening, each area, a defeat and its recovery, saving and Continue, optional puzzles, the Guardian, the ending and credits, and audio, and it lists the assumptions still waiting for the user's confirmation.
- [ ] Reviewer play check (manual, in a browser): the user plays the whole game once using the checklist and reports pacing, difficulty, and anything that felt unfair or confusing.

### What was built

- **Criterion 1 (Experience along the route).** `tests\main-route.ts` reads the intended route from the shipped content (the sand trail of the woods, the mountain and the cave, any open ground on the coast and in the castle, every door shut, the castle's route ending at the Guardian's Checkpoint) and what it meets and crosses; `tests\route-experience.test.ts` sums it against the thresholds: Level 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 at the exits of the coast, woods, mountain, cave and at the arena (the route's Experience, cumulative: 15, 33, 55, 75, 104; thresholds 10, 25, 45, 70, 100). The current data already met the targets, so **no number changed for this criterion**. The play-through that "feels right" is left to the manual check.
- **Criterion 3 (the Guardian can be beaten, and is not a pushover).** `tests\dodging-bot.ts` plays the fight at the Game core's interface and nothing else: it reads the view, sends player commands, and keeps to what a player sees. Each step it drinks a Healing Potion at half Health (through the inventory page, which pauses the game), steps out of any lit zone along the shortest straight run, walks up to the Guardian and strikes whenever the blow would land. Two settings stand for the player: `reactionMs`, how long it takes to act on a telegraph, and `missEvery`, one telegraph in so many goes unseen and so unanswered (the lapse; 0 is none, 1 is every one). `tests\profile-heroes.ts` puts a hero at the arena's gateway the way a save would (Level, Stat Point split, Gear, potions), in the shipped content without the ordinary monsters, and `tests\guardian-balance.test.ts` pins the relations (17 tests, about 3 s). The current numbers already met the criterion, so **no Guardian number changed**; the old "guesses for the tuning pass" comment in `monsters.ts` now says they were kept (comment only).

- **Criterion 2 (fights, Gear and supplies).** `tests\duel.ts` plays one fight through the core as a player plays who never dodges: a hero of a given make walks up to one shipped monster in an empty room and strikes whenever the blow would land; it drinks a Healing Potion, through the inventory page, at half its Health or less when it has one. `tests\fight-table.ts` runs it for every ordinary monster type, where the route first meets it, for three builds of the route hero (all Stat Points in Health, all in Attack, and a split dealt Attack, Health, Defense in turn), at the Level and with the Gear it has on coming into the area. A fight is *demanding* when the worst build loses half its Health or more without a potion. `tests\route-supplies.ts` steps each piece of Gear over the piece below it in its slot, and ledgers the Healing Potions and arrows area by area against what the route's fights take. `tests\fights.test.ts` (9 tests, about 3 s) pins: the demanding fights are exactly the three tougher variants (raider-veteran, cave-bear, castle-knight), not more than a quarter of the ordinary types, and every one of them costs some build a potion while no approachable fight costs any; every piece of Gear is a step of at least a fifth of the Attack or Defense it raises, at the Level it is found; the Gear within four tiles of the route is the Knight Mail, the Short Bow and the sword the hero began with (the kit of the Guardian tests), and the armor gets better in the mountain and again in the castle; the potions near the route carry an all-Attack hero and a split hero through every area and leave at least six for the Guardian; with every potion that lies without a door even an all-Health hero who never dodges is never short; and the arrows near the route cover the shots that take every ranged monster of the area off, twice over. **Three numbers changed** (data only, `src\content\monsters.ts`): `raider-charger` damage 7 to 6, `cave-lizard` damage 7 to 6, `castle-ghoul` damage 9 to 7 (their comments say so). ### Decisions and defaults

- **The intended route** is the shortest walk over the designed trail, as the area tests already take it, with every door and gate shut and no key held. The castle's leg ends at the Guardian's Checkpoint (65, 11), three tiles past the end of the castle test's route (62, 11); nothing is placed between them.
- **The reference heroes of the Guardian fight** are at Level 6 with five Stat Points spent in one of five splits (all in Attack, mostly Attack 1/3/1, balanced 2/2/1 as in the Guardian's own tests, all in Health, all in Defense). The *main-route* hero carries the Basic Sword, the Knight Mail and the Short Bow (the best of what lies within four tiles of the trail); the *optional* hero has the Katana, the Knight Mail and the Long Bow (the Steel Sword, the Chain Mail, the Long Bow and the Katana are behind side paths and puzzles). The *under-levelled* hero is Level 3 with 1/1/0 points, the Knight Mail and the Basic Sword.
- **What the tests pin, and what they only document.** The pins are relations: a hero that answers each telegraph within 600 ms is never hurt; one that misses a third of them still wins with the potions the route spares; the optional hero strikes fewer times and is never worse off than the main-route one at the same lapse; the Level 3 hero loses where the Level 6 one wins and needs more potions where both win. The measured breaking point (about 700 ms) and the damage tallies are written in `docs\balance.md`, not pinned, so a tuning that moves them by a little does not break a test.
- **The fight classes (criterion 2).** *Demanding* means that the worst of three builds loses half its Health or more in the plain fight (`DEMANDING_SHARE` in `tests\fight-table.ts`); the tougher variants are named in the test (the strongest type of each of the mountain, the cave and the castle, which the area tests already pin as at least twice the Health of the rest), and the Guardian has its own tests. The builds are all in Health, all in Attack and a split because those are the extremes a player can reach on the way; all in Defense is nearly invulnerable and is left out of the table (the Guardian tests have it). The hero is at the Level its route has earned by the *entrance* of the area (the least it has there, so the cost is not understated: it will level up in the area) with the Gear it has found within four tiles of the route in the areas before.
- **"Noticeable" is a fifth of the stat** (`isNoticeable`, whole-number arithmetic so that no rounding decides it). Each piece is compared with the next piece below it in its slot, at the Level of the area where it lies, because that is what a hero who takes it sees on the inventory page. The weakest steps today are the Katana over the Steel Sword for an all-Attack hero (Attack 5 to 6, exactly a fifth) and the Knight Mail over the Plate Armor (Defense 3 to 4 or 4 to 5, a third and a quarter), so the pin sits just under them.
- **Supplies "for a player who uses them sensibly"** means: the hero starts each area at full Health (the entrance Checkpoint), takes the potions that lie within four tiles of the trail (or, for the second pin, every one that lies without a door), drinks at half Health or less, and the ledger counts `floor((damage - max Health) / 5) + 1` potions for the total damage the area's fights take with no dodging. Luck is left out: monster drops (expected extra on the route: 0.3 potions on the coast, 0 in the woods, 0.5 in each of the mountain, the cave and the castle; arrows 0, 0.5, 0.75, 0.75, 1.5) are a bonus the pins do not count on.
- **The play-time target is not chased (criterion 4).** The guesses were written down before the estimate was first computed (low, typical and high of each, with a reason) and were not changed after; no factor was picked to land on 45 to 60. The estimate is a model, and the finding is that the adventure is about a third of the length that the spec asks for; the criterion's "about 45-60 minutes" is therefore not met and is left unticked, with the user's playtest (the clock time asked in the checklist) as the evidence for the decision between more content and a revised target. A guess that moves the estimate most is the puzzles (up to ten minutes in the high scenario), the looks at menus and the walking.
- **Where the whole-route walk ends (criterion 8).** At the Guardian's Checkpoint, on the same intended route as the Experience test (the castle's leg being the one that ends there). The fight itself is criterion 3's and the walk from the arena to the vessel is `the-harbor.test.ts`'s. The walk's game has no ordinary monster (`quietGameOf`), so it checks the route, the exits and the Checkpoints, not the fights; the hero spends each Stat Point on Health when the game asks, since the milestones and the route give Experience and the game waits for the choice.

### Assumptions

- **Six Healing Potions at the arena** (`POTIONS_SPARED`). The area tests put about 11 to 13 potions within four tiles of the trail (coast 3, woods 1 and a borderline chest, mountain 2, cave 2, castle 3); a hero that fought every monster and drank sensibly has used about half. This is the number the whole Guardian simulation leans on, and the first thing to confirm in the playtest.
- **A lapse is a fixed pattern, not chance.** `missEvery: 3` leaves every third telegraph unanswered. A person's mistakes come in bunches and at the wrong times, so this is a stand-in, chosen for being exactly repeatable. The reaction is counted from the first step of the wind-up, and the bot sees the lit zone the moment it appears.
- **The bot is melee-only.** It never shoots, so a hero who kites with the bow is not simulated; the arrows are checked as a supply instead (criterion 2). Drinking costs no game time, as in the game, where the page pauses it.
- **The hero begins at full Health at the Guardian's Checkpoint**, as after a Continue (the simulation starts from `continue` on a patched snapshot), in content without the ordinary monsters (the castle's own are not part of this fight).
- **The duel is a plain fight, not a player.** The hero starts a few tiles from the monster (one less than the distance the monster notices from, at least two), walks up and strikes, never steps aside and never shoots; a player who dodges a charger's dash or shoots a spitter pays less. So the class pins say "a hero who is careless loses under half its Health", which is stricter than the ticket needs. The class of a charger is sensitive to the start distance and to the hero standing in its lane, which is why three chargers needed a point or two.
- **An arrow hits one time in two** (`ARROWS_PER_HIT = 2`) when the arrows of the route are checked against the shots a ranged monster takes (`ceil(Health / arrow Attack)`, the arrow's Attack being the hero's with the bow it has by then).
- **The ledger of potions is a model, not a play-through**: it adds up the Health the area's fights take from duels with potions to spare, and treats a potion as exactly 5 Health because the hero drinks at half Health or less (at least 5 missing for a max Health of 10 or more). It does not see the order in which things are found, so it reads "enough over the area", not "at the right moment".
- **The play-time model (criterion 4)** counts only what the content gives and nine guesses. It leaves out the time in menus other than the looks counted in `pageSeconds`, saving, the audio controls, a defeat in any fight but the three demanding ones, and the time a player is away from the game. A Guardian attempt that fails is taken to last as long as the won one (the first tries are in `guardianAttempts`). The plain fights are the duel of the no-dodging hero (so a fight is as short as it can be, and `fightCare` carries what a person adds). The words on show (143) are the placeholder story, the ending and the credits, which are player-paced cards, being read once at `wordsPerMinute`. *Near the route* is the same four tiles as in the supplies.
