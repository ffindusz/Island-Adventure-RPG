# 19: The Guardian

**What to build:** The single, distinct undead Guardian that defends the castle, in an arena at the end of the castle route, with a Checkpoint immediately before it. The fight is a manageable climax with telegraphed attacks that can be avoided by positioning alone. Defeating the Guardian opens the way to the forgotten harbor. A defeated Guardian stays defeated; an undefeated Guardian starts fresh on every retry. Art stays placeholder until the art tickets.

**Blocked by:** 18: The old castle

**Status:** resolved

**Spec stories:** 16, 17, 54-56, 77, 86

- [x] A Checkpoint sits on the route immediately before the arena, with no ordinary monsters between it and the arena. The arena is part of the castle Adventure Area and replaces the plain wall that ended the route.
- [x] The Guardian is one distinct fight with at least two different telegraphed attack patterns (built from existing behaviors plus at least one pattern unique to it). Its numbers and patterns are content data, tuned for moderate difficulty with the Levels and Gear of the intended route.
- [x] Defeating the Guardian grants Experience and opens a gate to the harbor section, a minimal walkable stub that ticket 20 completes. The harbor is unreachable until the Guardian is defeated.
- [x] A defeated Guardian stays defeated through defeat recovery, backtracking, and Continue. An undefeated Guardian is reset to full Health and its starting state on each retry, whether after a defeat or after the hero leaves and returns.
- [x] The Guardian's state is part of the snapshot and the round-trip test from ticket 11.
- [x] Default, not in the spec and flagged for the user: the game also autosaves when the Guardian is defeated, so quitting right after the victory does not force a rematch.
- [x] Content validation requires exactly one Guardian, a Checkpoint immediately before its arena, and a harbor that is unreachable without defeating it.
- [x] Note for tuning: per the defeat rules, a defeat at the Guardian returns the hero to the Checkpoint before it and resets the ordinary monsters across the whole castle area.
- [x] Tests at the Game core's interface cover attack timing and avoidance, staying defeated, retry reset, the harbor gate, the autosave on victory, and each validation rule.
- [x] Reviewer play check (manual, in a browser): lose to the Guardian and confirm the return and reset, then win and confirm the harbor gate opens.

### What was built

- **The `guardian` behavior** (`src/core/guardian.ts`, `definitions.ts`, `content.ts`). A Guardian's type has `patterns`, a list of `sweep`, `charge` and `smite`, each with its damage, wind-up and recovery. The sweep is the chaser's blow on the zone round the body (`strike`, now exported from `chaser.ts`), the charge is the charger's dash (`launchDash` and `dash`, exported from `charger.ts`), and the smite is the pattern of its own: a square, `size` tiles wide, on the spot where the hero stood when the wind-up began. `MonsterState.nextPattern` (a Guardian's only) is the index of the pattern it tries first at its next decision (while a pattern is in progress it is already advanced); it is saved. The loader refuses a Guardian with fewer than two patterns or fewer than two kinds, and numbers that make no sense.
- **The gate and the fall** (`monster-damage.ts`, `devices.ts`, `device-content.ts`). A gate has `opensWhenDefeated`, the id of a Guardian of its own area. In the step that takes the Guardian's last Health the gate opens, for good, and the step tells `monster-hit`, `monster-defeated`, `guardian-defeated` (new in `interface.ts`, with the `monsterId`), then the Experience. No lever may name such a gate (the loader refuses it) and Interact does nothing to it.
- **Fought once** (`guardian-reset.ts`, called by `recoverHero` and `crossExits`). A Guardian that has not fallen is put back as the content placed it (full Health, its start tile, idle, its first pattern next) at every defeat and every exit from its area, in every area; the ordinary monsters of a Checkpoint's area are renewed as before, and a Guardian that has fallen stays fallen with its gate open, through a defeat, a walk out and back, and Continue.
- **What loading refuses** (`guardian-content.ts`, called from `loadContent`; the new `requireGuardian` flag of `ContentDefinition`). Always: a second Guardian; a gate that waits for no monster, for a monster of another area or for one that is no Guardian; a lever that names such a gate; a way to the Guardian's tile that needs a door or a gate (its tile is a destination of the route check, like an exit). With the flag, which the game's content sets: no Guardian at all; no Checkpoint immediately before it (on every way to it from the entrances and the start of its area, with no ordinary monster on the Guardian's side of it); no gate that opens when it is defeated; and an empty harbor (no walkable tile that the open gate leads to and nothing else does). `tilesBehindGuardianGates` returns those tiles; it is exported from its module, not from the core's index.
- **The castle** (`castle.ts`) is 92 by 30 tiles now (it was 64 by 30). The east wall of the last hall (column 63) is solid but for a mouth at (63, 11), the end of the route being still (62, 11). A gateway one tile wide and five long (row 11, columns 63 to 67) holds the second Checkpoint of the castle, `guardian-checkpoint` at (65, 11), and nothing else. The arena is sand, columns 68 to 83 and rows 7 to 15, with four pillars at (72, 9), (72, 13), (80, 9) and (80, 13), and the Guardian `arena-guardian` stands alone at (76, 11). In its far wall, at (84, 11), is the gate `harbor-gate` (`opensWhenDefeated: 'arena-guardian'`), shut, with no key and no lever. Behind it, the stub of the harbor: sand (columns 85 to 88, rows 10 to 12) and water (columns 89 and 90), enclosed by rock, with nothing placed in it. `src/content/index.ts` sets `requireGuardian: true`.
- **The Guardian** is `castle-guardian` in `monsters.ts`: 100 Health, size 1.6, 30 Experience, 2.2 tiles a second, notices from 7 tiles, no drops; patterns in the order sweep, charge, smite. All the numbers are guesses: see the tuning note.
- **The adapter, small**: the flat gold `0xffd700` in `MONSTER_COLOURS` (`art.ts`); the notice GUARDIAN DEFEATED / THE GATE OPENED (kind `gate-opened` in `notice.ts`, set by `feedback.ts` on `guardian-defeated`); `guardian-defeated` in `SAVING_EVENTS` of `autosave.ts`, with its comment saying why; and the checks of `saved-game.ts` (a Guardian keeps `nextPattern` and no other monster does; its actions; a gate that its fall opens is open if and only if it has fallen).
- **Tests**: six new files, 151 tests: `guardian-patterns` (25), `guardian-gate` (14), `guardian-retry` (12), `guardian-content` (25), `guardian-validation` (35) and `the-guardian` (40, the shipped content), with `guardian-fixtures.ts`. The saved-game fixture (`saved-adventure.ts`) has a Guardian and its gate, so `saved-game.test.ts`, `save-round-trip.test.ts` and the sweeps cover the new state. **README**: a section, The Guardian, and phrase-level edits in the sections it touches.

### Decisions and defaults

- **Flagged for the user (a default, not in the spec): the game autosaves when the Guardian is defeated.** No Checkpoint follows the victory, so without it a tab closed right after would bring back the Checkpoint before the arena with the Guardian alive and its gate shut. The slot then holds the game where the hero stands in the arena, with the Guardian gone and the gate open.
- **Three patterns, two of them the older behaviors' attacks** (the ticket: existing behaviors plus at least one pattern of its own): the sweep, the charge, and the smite. The Guardian takes them in turn, the first that reaches the hero from the one after the last it made; when none reaches, it walks toward the hero. Nothing is random, so a player can learn the order, and nothing keeps a hero out of the fight by standing off: the smite (range 5) is made at any hero within reach of it, and the Guardian walks to one beyond.
- **Every attack can be avoided by positioning alone** (the ticket): the zone, the lane and the square are fixed in the step the wind-up begins and are shown in the view; the charge's direction is pinned then too, so it goes down the lane whether or not the hero is still in it; the smite's square does not follow the hero; a hit on the Guardian never interrupts a wind-up, so the fight is won by getting out of the way and striking in the recovery, not by cutting an attack short. Tests pin the hero's time to step out at no more than half of each wind-up, and a recovery of at least two cooldowns of the hero's blow.
- **The reset is on every defeat and every exit, in every area**, as the ticket says (a defeat, or the hero leaving and returning). A defeat at the Guardian also renews the ordinary monsters across the castle, as the ticket's note for tuning says (the rule is the old one, and `guardian-retry` pins it); all of them lie west of the gateway, so a retry from the Checkpoint never meets them.
- **The Checkpoint is in the gateway, three tiles from the arena's first column**, and no way goes round it (the gateway is one tile wide). The Guardian stands at (76, 11) so that everything it can notice lies within the arena: a hero at the Checkpoint is not noticed, and the way back after a defeat is a short walk (the hero's 5 tiles a second: about half a second to the arena, about 2 seconds to the Guardian).
- **Several Checkpoints may lie before an arena** in other content: the validation accepts any one that lies on every way and has no ordinary monster on the Guardian's side, and names the nearest when none does. The harbor is whatever only the open gate leads to, so that content may build it as it likes; the check does not say how big (the shipped test wants 8 open tiles at least, and nothing placed in it, until ticket 20).
- **The arena has room to dodge in**: 16 by 9 tiles of sand with four single-tile pillars (the shipped test wants 12 by 9 at least, and no more than a tenth of it solid).
- **30 Experience, no drops.** A hero who fought the whole route has 104 and the count stops at 100, so the Guardian adds nothing to it; a hero at 70 (Level 5) is taken to Level 6 by it (a test). The README says so.
- **The Guardian has a gold of its own, a placeholder** (`0xffd700`): the castle is not in `FINAL_ART_AREAS` (ticket 25), and a test keeps the colour apart from every monster, the sand and the missing-colour magenta.
- **`SNAPSHOT_VERSION` is 3** (it was 2, with the literal of `Snapshot.version`) because `MonsterState.nextPattern` is a new saved field: without it the rotation of a Guardian would start again at every Continue. As ever, an older saved game is named incompatible, so a player who has a slot from before this ticket starts a New Game (ticket 13 did the same, 1 to 2). The checks in `saved-game.ts` and the fixture were extended in the same commits, as the README asks.
- **`requireGuardian` is off unless the content says it**, so that the small maps of the tests, which have no Guardian, still load; a second Guardian is refused either way.
- No ADR written; three are proposed below. `GLOSSARY.md` is untouched; the terms missing from it are listed below.
- The spec and the ticket do not disagree on anything this ticket touches.

### Tuning note

Every number of the Guardian is a **guess for ticket 26 to balance**, worked out by arithmetic against reference heroes and read back from the real game in `tests/the-guardian.test.ts` (the table `HEROES` there).

- The hero comes to the Guardian at Level 6 (104 Experience on the route, 100 counted): 5 Stat Points, base Health 10, Attack 1, Defense 1; a Stat Point is worth 2 Health, 1 Attack or 1 Defense. A hero has 5 tiles a second, a blow every 0.4 s, a melee reach of 0.75 and a minimum damage of 1; the Guardian walks at 2.2.
- **Hero A**, Level 6 (2 Health, 2 Attack, 1 Defense), the Steel Sword and the armor it began with: Health 14, a strike of 4, Defense 2. The sweep takes 6 from it, the charge 9, the smite 5: it survives 2 sweeps, 1 charge or 2 smites at full Health, and no more. The Guardian takes 25 of its strikes.
- **Hero B**, the same Level and points with the Katana and the Knight Mail (the best the island holds): a strike of 5, Defense 5. It takes 3, 6 and 2, and survives 4 sweeps, 2 charges or 6 smites. 20 strikes. (In the smoke a hero in the Knight Mail took 3, 6 and 2, as the table says.)
- **Hero C**, Level 4 (45 Experience, one point of each), the Steel Sword: Health 12, a strike of 3, Defense 2. It takes 6, 9 and 5, and survives 1 sweep, 1 charge or 2 smites. 34 strikes: the least a player who hurried might be, and a fight that is long but not a wall. A test holds every hero between 12 and 40 strikes.
- **Hero D**, Level 5 at 70 Experience: the Guardian's 30 takes it to Level 6.
- **The patterns against the hero's time**: the hero can step out of the sweep's zone in 0.28 s (the wind-up is 0.9 s), out of the charge's lane in 0.235 s (1.0 s), out of the smite's square in 0.375 s (1.1 s), each at most half of the wind-up (tests). The recoveries are 1.1 s, 2.2 s and 1.4 s, each at least two cooldowns of the hero's blow (0.8 s, tests), and a turn of the three patterns takes 7.7 s from the first wind-up to the end of the last recovery. A hero who dodges every attack and strikes in the recoveries lands perhaps five or six strikes in a turn, once the stepping out and the walking back are taken off, so hero A (25 strikes) needs four or five turns: 40 to 60 s with the Guardian's own walking. That is an estimate, not a measurement.
- **Not measured**: how much of the Guardian a bow can take off from afar. An arrow adds the bow's bonus (+1 the short bow, +2 the long bow) to the hero's Attack, the stock on the island is limited, the Guardian is slower than the hero, and the smite (range 5) and the charge (a lane of 7) are what reach a hero who backs away. Ticket 26 should play it, and a scratch bot over `sceneOf` would measure the length of the fight.
- **What a blow leaves**: hero A at full Health has 8 of 14 after a sweep and 5 of 14 after the charge, the hardest blow; a Healing Potion restores 5.

### How to run it

- `npm.cmd run dev`, open the URL with `?debug`, Enter on the title and Enter on the card of the opening. To see the end of the route without walking it, take `gameCore.snapshot()`, patch `state.areaId` (`'castle'`) and `state.hero.position` (`{ x: 63.5, y: 11.5 }` is the mouth of the gateway, `{ x: 65.5, y: 11.5 }` the Checkpoint, `{ x: 75, y: 11.5 }` beside the Guardian), with `state.hero.facing` `'right'`, and `gameCore.send({ type: 'continue', snapshot })`; past 10 Experience also patch `state.hero.statPoints` to match the Level. To skip the long fight, set the `health` of `arena-guardian` in `state.areas.castle.monsters` to 8 first. Walking east through the gateway into the arena starts the fight; the gate is the picture in the east wall of the arena, at the middle.
- `npx.cmd vitest run tests/guardian-patterns.test.ts tests/guardian-gate.test.ts tests/guardian-retry.test.ts tests/guardian-content.test.ts tests/guardian-validation.test.ts tests/the-guardian.test.ts` runs the new tests; add `tests/the-castle.test.ts tests/shipped-enemies.test.ts tests/saved-game.test.ts tests/save-round-trip.test.ts` for the ones it changed. `npm.cmd run check` is the gate.
