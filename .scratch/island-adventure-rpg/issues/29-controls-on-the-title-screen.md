# 29: Controls on the title screen

**What to build:** The title screen's menu gets a third line, Controls, under New Game and Continue. Choosing it shows the same list of keys as the Controls page in play (ticket 28), and Esc brings the menu back with the cursor on Controls, so that a player can read every key before starting a game. The keys of the sound keep working while the list is shown, as they do on every screen.

**Blocked by:** 28: A Controls page in play

**Status:** resolved

**Spec stories:** 109

- [x] The menu has the lines New Game, Continue and Controls, top to bottom. Controls can always be chosen, whether or not there is a saved game; Continue is still greyed when there is none. The cursor starts where it started before (on Continue when there is a saved game to continue from, otherwise on New Game), and the keys that move through the menu (W and S, the arrows) move it over the lines that can be chosen.
- [x] Enter on Controls shows the list of keys of ticket 28: the same list from the same tables, so that the title and the page in play cannot disagree. Esc closes it, and so does Enter, and the menu is as it was with the cursor on Controls. Choosing Controls starts no game, writes no save and never asks about replacing one.
- [x] While the list is shown nothing else of the menu answers: Enter does not start a game and the question whether to replace the saved game cannot open. The keys of the sound (N, - and +) work as they do everywhere.
- [x] Showing and closing the list are heard as a page opening and closing, as the question whether to replace the saved game is.
- [x] The rules are in the pure part of the title and are tested directly (ADR 0005): the cursor's start and its moves over three lines with and without a saved game; Enter on Controls shows the list; Esc and Enter close it; nothing else answers while it shows; New Game over a saved game still asks first and Esc still keeps the saved game.
- [x] The list is whole on the 320 x 192 screen. It may take the whole screen while it shows, so long as the player can tell from it that it is the controls of the game.
- [x] The title fits in each of its states (no saved game, a saved game, the question about replacing it, saving not available or a damaged save, and a save from another version): the window is clear of the name and above the foot of the screen, and no row of it is covered by the line of the audio. The new line makes the window 15 pixels taller, which the tallest states have no room for (see the comments), so something else gives: hints that share a row, or fewer rows of message. That is the implementer's call. The hand-worked heights in `tests/audio-line.test.ts` are updated to the rows the title draws, and no test of room is weakened.
- [x] The README's controls table says that the title offers the list (Up and Down choose New Game, Continue or Controls; Esc and Enter close the list), and its text about the title mentions the Controls line.
- [x] The type check, the tests, and the build pass (`npm.cmd run check`).
- [ ] Reviewer play check (manual, in a browser): open the game with no saved game and see three lines, the cursor on New Game, and Controls that can be chosen; choose it and see the list whole, press N and - while it shows (the sound answers), then Esc (the menu is back, the cursor on Controls). Do the same with a saved game (the cursor starts on Continue; New Game still asks before it replaces the save). Look at the title in each state that the README tells how to make (a damaged save, a save from another version) and see that the window, the name and the line of the audio do not overlap.

## Comments

Added at the player's request ("we should add all the keyboard shortcuts somewhere for the user"), with the answer "in play and on the title screen". Spec story 109; the spec's key list says that the title offers the list as a Controls entry in its menu.

Assumed, not decided with the player: the list is a line of the menu and not a key of its own (H opens the page in play only, and the title does not read it), and Enter closes the list as well as Esc, because Enter is how it was opened.

For the implementer: `src/adapter/title-menu.ts` is pure and holds the rules (`MENU_ENTRIES`, `openMenu`, `selectableEntries`, `pressMenu`). Its `choice` is what starts a game, so the list is better a state of the menu, like `confirming`, than a choice. `title-scene.ts` draws the rows (`rowsFor`) in a window 200 wide, which is narrower than the list will want.

The room, worked out from `tests/audio-line.test.ts` and `src/adapter/title-layout.ts`: the room under the name is 152 pixels (from row 36 to row 188), and the line of the audio in the bottom right corner begins at row 169, so the rows of the window may end there and no later, which makes 139 pixels the tallest the window can be. Today it is 102 (normal), 57 (the question), 128 (saving not available or a damaged save) and 139 (a save from another version). A third line of 15 pixels makes them 117, 57, 143 and 154: the last two are too tall, by 4 and 15 pixels. The four hint rows (7 pixels high with 4 between them) are the cheapest place to find that: two of them that share a row each give back 11.

**What was built (ticket 29).**
- `src/adapter/title-menu.ts` (pure): `MENU_ENTRIES` is New Game, Continue, Controls. `TitleMenu` gains `showingControls`, a state of the menu beside `confirming` (the question and the list are never both open). `TitleChoice` is still New Game, Continue or null: Controls is not a choice, so showing the list cannot start a game. `selectableEntries` always holds Controls and holds Continue only over a saved game, so the cursor keys step over a greyed Continue, and `openMenu` still starts the cursor on Continue or on New Game. `pressMenu`: Enter on Controls shows the list (no question, no choice); while it shows, Esc and Enter, alone or together, close it with the cursor on Controls, and nothing else changes the menu. New `soundOfPress(before, after, choice)`: `confirm` when a choice starts a game, `page-open` or `page-close` when the question or the list opens or closes, nothing for a press that did neither.
- `src/adapter/title-window.ts` (new, pure, no Phaser): `layoutTitleWindow(menu, saved)` is the window of the title as data: its rows (text, a line of the menu with its colour and whether it is highlighted, or the list), each with its box in screen pixels, the window's left, top, width and height, and whether the name of the game is drawn. It holds what `rowsFor` in `title-scene.ts` held, where no test could reach it, and the new rows.
- `src/adapter/title-scene.ts` is now a shell: it draws what `layoutTitleWindow` says and plays what `soundOfPress` says (the rule that the scene had inline for the question moved into the pure part). The list is `layoutControlsList(controlsList())` drawn by `drawControlsList` (ticket 28), the same table and the same drawing as the Controls page in play; a test pins that the list row of the title equals the one that `panelFor` gives for the open page.
- The list while it shows: a window of 252 x 181 at (34, 5), the size and the place of the page in play's window, headed CONTROLS, with `ESC OR ENTER TO CLOSE` at its foot (made of `closePageKeyLabel()` and `confirmKeyLabel()`). The name of the game is left out: the 152 pixels under it cannot hold 181.
- `src/adapter/title-layout.ts`: only the comment of `TITLE_LINE_CORNER`.
- Documentation, in the same commits as the code: the README (the controls table; the paragraph on the title with the Controls line, the list, the rules, the heights and the squeeze; ticket 28's "the title screen can draw it in one of its own" now says that it does; the paragraphs on the tests) and `docs/playtest-checklist.md` (the checks of the opening and of every title state; "H shows the controls" no longer says "(the next ticket)": the Controls line of the title is how the page is found; the Controls line itself is one more of the "Assumptions still waiting for you").

**The squeeze, and the height of every state.** The room is as the comment above works it out: the rows may end at row 169, where the line of the audio begins, so the window is 139 high at most (its rows and 12 of padding above and under). Rows are 7 high (text) or 15 (a line of the menu), with 4 between rows and none between two lines of the menu; a space is 4 + 0 + 4.

- The question: 7 + 4 + 0 + 4 + 7 + 4 + 7 = 33, and 57 (unchanged).
- The list: 7 + 4 + 0 + 4 + 127 + 4 + 0 + 4 + 7 = 157, and 181 with the padding; 252 wide, so (320 - 252) / 2 = 34 from the left and (192 - 181) / 2 = 5 from the top. Its last row, the hint, is 125 wide and ends at x = 222 where the line of the audio, at its widest, begins at x = 233: no row is under the line, and the line covers only the empty corner of the window's frame (as the line covers the top right corner of the page in play).
- What gave: both things the ticket names. (1) The two hints of the sound share a row, `N TO MUTE   - OR + FOR VOLUME` (29 characters, 173 of the 176 pixels of room, three spaces between the halves, made from `audioKeyLabel`), so the hints are three rows (W/S OR ARROWS TO CHOOSE, ENTER TO SELECT, that one): 11 pixels back in every state that has hints. (2) A save from another version is told in two rows of message like a damaged one, `SAVE IS FROM ANOTHER VERSION.` (in the colour of a warning) and `A NEW GAME WILL REPLACE IT.`; the row `SAVED GAME CANNOT BE USED.` is gone because the greyed Continue says it: 11 pixels back there. Either alone leaves that state at 143, 4 over the room; both give 132. The messages about saving not available and a damaged save are as they were.

**Decisions and defaults.**
- The list is a state of the menu, not a choice (as the ticket's comment suggests); Esc and Enter both close it, and the cursor stays on Controls. The title does not read H.
- The heading and the hint are mine: CONTROLS, and `ESC OR ENTER TO CLOSE`, which names the keys the title answers and not H (a test pins both). The list takes the whole screen and the name of the game is left out, so what the player sees is plainly the list of the controls.
- With no saved game, Down from New Game goes over the greyed Continue to Controls; with one, the cursor starts on Continue and Down goes on to Controls.
- The question and the list are heard as the same two sounds of a page, `page-open` and `page-close`; `soundOfPress` is now the one place that says so, and the scene plays what it says.
- The hint with the keys of the sound has three spaces between its halves; seen in the screenshots, it does not look cramped. A different wording or gap is a change to `menuRows` in `title-window.ts` alone, if the height is kept.
