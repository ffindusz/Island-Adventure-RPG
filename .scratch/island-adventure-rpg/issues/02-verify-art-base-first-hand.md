# 02: Verify the art base first-hand

**What to build:** A written, first-hand check of the art base before the project commits to it. The provisional base is the CC0 Ninja Adventure pack (pixel-boy, itch.io), but public text could not confirm that it covers every Adventure Area or a hero with both sword and bow. Inspect the actual files and report what the game needs, what the pack covers, what is missing, and how to fill each gap; if the pack fails, recommend a fallback. Gameplay tickets proceed on placeholder art in the meantime, so this ticket gates only the art tickets (22-25).

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Download permission:** On 2026-10-06 the user allowed downloading the provisional base pack (Ninja Adventure, free) for this inventory, replacing the earlier "previews only; do not download". Not covered: any purchase or paid asset, and downloading any other pack. Fallbacks and the shipwreck supplement are researched through their public pages and previews only; ask before downloading them. The agent does the inventory, but whether the art is acceptable is the user's judgment, so the last criterion is completed by the user, not the agent. Downloaded files stay outside the repository; later art tickets copy in only what they use, each with a licence record entry.

**Spec stories:** 94-96, 99-102

- [ ] The pack is downloaded only as permitted above, to a location outside the repository, and nothing is purchased. If the download cannot be scripted without working around an access control, the agent stops and gives the user exact manual steps instead.
- [ ] A coverage matrix marks each need as found, partial, or missing: terrain for the shipwreck coast, woods, mountain, cave, and old castle with harbor; one hero with walk, sword, and bow animations; monsters for every Adventure Area including undead; a distinct Guardian; chests, Ammunition caches, Healing Potions, keys, doors, and levers; HUD and page art; arrow and effect sprites; the tile size; and any bundled music and sound effects.
- [ ] The licence is confirmed from the pack's own licence text or page (not from a summary), with source and date. Every other asset source (the shipwreck supplement, any audio) has a confirmed licence that allows public and commercial use. All of this goes into a single asset licence record (each asset's source, licence, date checked, and required attribution text) that later tickets extend and that the credits screen draws from.
- [ ] Each gap has a concrete plan: the small, visually matching shipwreck supplement (a verified-licence source, or composition from existing pieces), and sword plus bow on one hero (for example, borrowed animations). If the pack is rejected, a fallback is recommended from the candidates already researched (TinyRPG Collection, Mithrillor Pixel RPG Characters Pack, VEXED "Another Perspective") with price, licence, and redistribution terms.
- [ ] The result is recorded as a short decision record, and the working tile size assumed in ticket 01 is confirmed or the needed change is noted.
- [ ] The user reviews the result and approves the art base or picks a fallback; the decision is recorded.
