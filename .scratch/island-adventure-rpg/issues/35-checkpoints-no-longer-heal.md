# 35: A Checkpoint no longer restores Health

**What to build:** After playing the third build the player found the game too easy because every Checkpoint refills the hero's Health. Asked how far to go, the player chose that none of the six Checkpoints heals, that defeat still returns the hero to the last Checkpoint at full Health, and that the Healing Potions are left as they are. In the Game core a Checkpoint stops setting the hero's Health to its maximum: it still emits `checkpoint-activated`, still records the recovery point and the supplies that defeat gives back, and still makes the game save, but the Health the hero walked in with is the Health it keeps, and it carries from one Adventure Area to the next until a Healing Potion, a level-up or a defeat changes it. The notice that says HEALTH FULL when a Checkpoint is touched is false now and goes. The balance of the shipped route was measured on the assumption that every area begins at full Health (the ledger of potions in `tests/route-supplies.ts`, the claims of `tests/fights.test.ts` and `docs/balance.md`): that is re-based on the Health the hero carries, and a claim that no longer holds is changed to what is true or dropped, never rigged to pass. No potion is added or moved, and no monster is changed, in this ticket.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** resolved

**Spec stories:** 115, 49, 78

## Comments

Added at the player's request after playing the third build. The player wrote "we should remove the health reset when entering a location, it makes it too easy (Checkpoint health)". Asked how far to go, the player chose that all six Checkpoints stop healing, that defeat still returns the hero at full Health, and that the potions are left as they are. Spec story 115 was written for it, and stories 49 and 78 are marked as superseded in part.

Decided with the player:

- Every one of the six Checkpoints (the coast's, the woods', the mountain's, the cave's and the castle's two) stops restoring Health. A Checkpoint still records the recovery point and the supplies, and the game still saves there.
- Defeat still returns the hero to the last Checkpoint at full Health.
- The Healing Potions are left as they are: none is added or moved, and a potion still restores 5.
- A harder route is the point. The route must be winnable by a hero that dodges; it need not be by one that does not.

Assumed, not decided with the player:

- That the notice loses its second line, HEALTH FULL, since it would be false, and that the implementer chooses the words that are true.
- That defeat staying a full heal is accepted, with its consequence: a player can lose a fight on purpose to be whole again. It follows from the player's choice, and no cost is added here. It is also why a hero can never be stuck with no Health and no potion: defeat is the way out.
- That the Guardian's fixtures stay as they are: the bot begins at full Health at the Guardian's Checkpoint and `POTIONS_SPARED` is what it has. The balance document says that the route may not bring a hero there like that.
- That a game saved under the old rules continues as it was saved, with the Health it holds.
- That the new game, the level-up rule and the Healing Potion are not touched.

Not decided: whether a build that the new numbers show cannot finish the route needs potions added or moved (the player left them as they are, so a finding goes to the player and nothing is changed here); whether the HUD or the notice should say more about Health at a Checkpoint.
