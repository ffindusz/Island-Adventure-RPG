# 13: Light puzzles and clues

**What to build:** Optional, light obstacles: keys found elsewhere that open locked doors, levers that open gates or side rooms, and readable clues (signs and inscriptions) read with E. The main route is never blocked by any of them, and content validation proves it. Puzzle state persists through defeat, area changes, and saves.

**Blocked by:** 11: Title screen, autosave, and Continue

**Status:** resolved

**Spec stories:** 12, 28, 29

- [x] Keys are collectable and shown in the inventory page. A locked door opens with its matching key (default: the key is consumed). Keys and opened doors are one-time loot, not consumables, so they persist through defeat.
- [x] Levers, used with E, toggle gates or doors. A lever's state persists through defeat, area changes, and Continue.
- [x] Pressing E at a sign or inscription shows short clue text (content data) that does not pause play for long.
- [x] Content validation rejects a device whose target does not exist, a key with no matching door, and any content in which an area's exit, the Checkpoints, or the route's required destinations cannot be reached without using a key, lever, or door.
- [x] The placeholder areas include a key-and-door side room and a lever-and-gate side room that demonstrate this.
- [x] Device state is included in the snapshot and the round-trip test from ticket 11.
- [x] Tests at the Game core's interface cover key and door use, lever toggling, persistence through defeat and a snapshot round trip, and each validation failure.
- [x] Reviewer play check (manual, in a browser): find a key and open its door, pull a lever, read a sign, then die, continue, and confirm the states remain.

### Decisions and defaults

- **A key is one-time loot, taken by walking onto it and consumed by its door.** The hero takes a key when its body overlaps it (`collectKeys`, right after `collectPickups`) and holds it in `hero.keys`; the door it opens takes it away for good, and the door stays open. Each key opens exactly one door and each door has a key that some area places: the loader refuses a door whose key no area places, a key no door needs and a key that two doors need, over all the areas together, because a key may lie in another area than its door. A hero without the key gets `door-locked` with the name of the key, and nothing changes; an open door does nothing.
- **A lever flips what it names, in its own area only.** E on a lever flips it (pulled, or put back) and flips the `open` of every door and gate it names: a shut one opens and an open one shuts. So a door that its key opened can be shut again by a lever and opened by the lever once more, and no hero is locked out. A lever that names nothing, names one twice, or names an id that is not a door or a gate of its own area is refused at load, so the target is always loaded with it.
- **A gate has no key and no E of its own**: it starts shut and only a lever moves it. A lever and a sign are solid always, pulled or not. There is no separate kind for an inscription: it is a sign with its own text, which ticket 23 can draw as a stone.
- **A sign is read with E and uses the text of an area.** Its text replaces what shows in the banner and lasts as long as the text of an area (`areaTextSteps`); nothing pauses, nothing is paged, and reading it again shows it again from the start. A sign keeps no state. The core cannot know the font, so a test over the shipped content checks that the pixel font draws every letter of every sign and area text.
- **One rule for what is in the way.** A shut door or gate stops the hero, the monsters, the hero's arrows, a charger's dash and the projectiles of a ranged monster, through `stopsWalkers` (where the chests already stood); an open one stops none of them. `device-blocking.test.ts` pins each of those.
- **Keys, doors, levers and gates stay through a defeat**, the opposite of an Ammunition cache: `consumable-rollback.ts` names each new kind and leaves it alone. They stay through area changes and are in the snapshot.
- **The saved shape changed, so `SNAPSHOT_VERSION` is 2**, in the same commit as the validator and the fixture, as ticket 11's README says. In `saved-game.ts`, `FLAG_OF` has the flag of each kind (`key` and `pickup`: `collected`; `door` and `gate`: `open`; `lever`: `pulled`; `sign`: none); `hero.keys` is checked against the ids of the keys of the content; a key that is held has to be marked as taken where its area placed it, or the save is damaged; a device that the content lacks, or a state of the wrong kind, makes the save incompatible, and wrong flags make it damaged. Ticket 14 changed no shape, so this is the only bump.
- **The route rule (criterion 4).** For each area, from each entrance (and from the start in the first area), an exit or a Checkpoint that the hero could reach with every door and gate open has to be reachable with every one shut: the hero finds them shut, and a key or a lever is never counted as a way through, so a route may not need either. What cannot be reached even with everything open is not this check's to say (the map's business), and content with no door or gate is never refused. The destinations are the exits and the Checkpoints, as the brief says; a later ticket that adds a required destination (the Guardian's arena, the vessel) adds it to `destinationsOf`. A milestone is not one: a milestone in a room behind a door is an optional reward.
- **The notices** (placeholder words, `notice.ts`): "LOCKED" over "NEEDS BRASS KEY" (the key's name), "DOOR OPENED" over "USED BRASS KEY", "LEVER PULLED" or "LEVER PUT BACK" over "SOMETHING MOVED", and "FOUND BRASS KEY" over "IT OPENS A DOOR", with the icon of the key.
- **The inventory page** lists the keys under the potions, two rows, then "+N MORE", so that the right column keeps Health, Attack and Defense inside 320 by 192.

### How a closed door or gate counts in the shipped-content tests (ticket 14's question)

- `tests/routes.ts`: `openTilesOf(content, area, doorsOpen = false)` counts a chest, a lever and a sign as solid always, and a door and a gate as solid while shut, which is how the hero finds them. `shipped-content` (the walk out of the first area) and `shipwreck-coast` use it as it is, with every door and gate shut, and neither of those areas has one; `shipped-enemies` never looks at interactables, so it holds as it did. `routes.test.ts` pins the helper: each kind, with the doors and gates counted shut and open, a key and a pickup as open ground, and bare floor, rock and the outside of the map.
- `shipped-areas.test.ts` asks for `doorsOpen = true` in one check only, that the hero can walk from the first entrance to every tile of an area that is not solid, because the rooms behind a door and a gate are meant to be reached by a hero who solved the puzzle. That no exit or Checkpoint needs a door or gate is the loader's check (`device-routes.test.ts`), and `shipped-puzzles.test.ts` pins that the key and the lever are reachable with everything shut. Its other checks keep the doors shut: room to stand beside every chest, and nothing of any kind on an entrance.
