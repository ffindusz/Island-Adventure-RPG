# 10: Automap

**What to build:** M opens the Automap page, pausing the game. It shows the whole island route (all Adventure Areas in order with their known connections), reveals detailed terrain only as the hero explores, marks the hero's position on the current area, and marks exits the hero has found. There are no quest markers and no map item to find. Discoveries persist through defeat.

**Blocked by:** 09: Checkpoints and defeat recovery

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Spec stories:** 85, 87-91

- [x] Each Adventure Area starts undiscovered. Terrain is recorded automatically as the hero explores it (a tunable reveal distance around the hero), and only then.
- [x] The page shows every area on the route in order with its connections, highlights the current area, marks the hero's position, and marks exits that have been found. There are no quest markers.
- [x] M opens and Esc closes the page, and the game is paused while it is open.
- [x] Discoveries persist through defeat and Checkpoint roll-back, and are part of the Game core's serializable state.
- [x] The Automap is driven by area content, so areas added later appear without rule changes.
- [x] Tests at the Game core's interface cover no reveal before exploring, reveal on exploring, hero position data, exits marked only once found, and persistence through defeat.
- [ ] Reviewer play check (manual, in a browser): explore part of an area, open the map, leave and re-enter, die and return, and confirm the discoveries remain.

## Comments

### What was built

- Core: `src/core/automap.ts` holds the rules (`revealAroundHero`, and `automapPage`, the page's view). Each area keeps `revealed`, a `[row][col]` grid of booleans, in its `AreaState`; the content gets `areaOrder` (the order of `areas`) and the tunable `automapRevealDistance`; the page `automap` joins `PAGE_IDS`, with `AutomapPageView` and `AutomapAreaView` in the interface. The reveal is called from four places: a new game and the end of each walk (`game.ts`), an arrival through an exit (`crossExits`) and the end of `recoverHero`.
- Adapter: `automap-layout.ts` (pure numbers, no Phaser: the layout of the route, the links, the pixels of a map, the found exits, the hero's mark, the cut names) and `automap-page.ts` (`AutomapPage`, which draws the page from the view on two Graphics layers of its own, and blinks the mark). Small hooks: `M` in `player-input.ts`, `automapPage.draw` in `play-scene.ts`, no panel for the Automap in `overlay.ts`, and `terrainColour` exported by `art.ts`.
- Content: the shipped content says `automapRevealDistance: 4`. No area changed.
- README: a new section, "The Automap", the M row in the controls, and the pages text and the layout brought up to date.
- 98 new tests of mine in three files: `automap.test.ts` (39, all at the Game core's interface), 3 more in `shipped-content.test.ts` (the real route), and `automap-layout.test.ts` (56, the pure layout). The branch has 783 tests in 41 files (the base had 506 in 29; the rest came with ticket 12).

### Decisions and defaults

- **Reveal shape: a disc of tiles.** Tile `(c, r)` is revealed when `(c - hc)² + (r - hr)² <= d²`, where `hc` and `hr` are the column and row of the hero's tile and `d` is the reveal distance in tiles. The loops are cut to the map. There is no line of sight: walls and water beside the hero are revealed like ground. `automapRevealDistance` is on the content (default 4, the shipped content says 4; 0 or more, a negative number or NaN is refused). A tile only ever goes from false to true.
- **When the first reveal happens.** At a new game (before the start area is entered), after the hero has walked in each fixed step (right after `walkHero`, before the milestones), as the hero arrives through an exit (the end of `crossExits`, the early-return path of `game.ts`) and after a defeat, once the hero is put back (the end of `recoverHero`). It is not in `enterArea`, because `recoverHero` enters the area before it has placed the hero (mutations M14b and M14c pin both). Never while a page is open or a level-up waits, because `step()` advances nothing then. `continue` reveals nothing: the snapshot carries the record. No new events: the view is the truth.
- **A found exit** is one with any tile of its `zone` revealed, worked out when the view is built (no state of its own, and nothing to use the exit first). Under a reveal distance below 1 an exit can be crossed before it is found; `definitions.ts` says so.
- **An undiscovered area** has no name (`name: null`), no terrain and no hero in the view, but it is on the route from the start, with its true size and its `connections`: the player sees that more is to come. It is drawn as a dark box named UNKNOWN. `discovered` means any tile of it is revealed.
- **The route** is `content.areaOrder`, a new field, because `Object.values` of the areas would reorder ids that look like integers. `connections` are the ids the area's exits lead to, each once, in the order of the exits. The page's view is built only while the page is open (`view()` still has no side effects).
- **Defeat** leaves the discoveries alone by construction: `recoverHero` and `consumable-rollback.ts` were not touched except for the reveal at the end of `recoverHero`. Tests pin it for a defeat in the Checkpoint's own area and for one that sends the hero to another area. Ticket 12's defeat paths (a charger's dash, a projectile) all end in `recoverHero`, so they reveal around the Checkpoint too (not tested separately).
- **`SNAPSHOT_VERSION` stays 1.** `revealed` is a new field of `AreaState`, so a snapshot made by an earlier build has none: ticket 11 has to bump the version or migrate (see Suggestions). `copyState` is a JSON deep copy, so the record is in every snapshot with no extra code, and a test pins that a game continued from a snapshot shows the same Automap, and the same open page.
- **Criterion 5** is pinned by a three-area route in `automap.test.ts` (arena, cellar, attic): the third area appears on the page with no rule change. The page it builds is a pure projection of the content and the state.
- **The page opens only when the game is not paused and no other page is open** (as for the stat page and the inventory): it does not open over a level-up that waits or over another page, and another page does not open over it. Only `close-page` with the Automap's id closes it.
- **The Automap draws itself.** `AutomapPage` has its own Graphics layers (depth 200 and 201, the same place as the overlay) and the overlay shows nothing for it, not even the PAUSED notice (as with the other pages). Ticket 22 has not landed in integration, so the page has a plain window of its own: panel, border and text in the colours the overlay uses today (a copy of its look, as its constants are private). Restyle `drawWindow` with 22's `UiLayer` frames when it lands: the map and the mark are drawn apart from the window, so only the window changes. The page uses only `drawText`, `TEXT_HEIGHT`, `textWidthOf` and `Graphics` of `pixel-font.ts`, which 22 keeps, and the layout takes the text widths as parameters, so a new font adapts.
- **The look.** Each area is a card: its name above its map, the revealed terrain in the terrain colours (`terrainColour`, exported by `art.ts`, so the page and the world agree), the current area ringed in gold with a gold name, found exits as pale patches (at least 3 by 3 pixels), and the hero as a mark that blinks gold and white every 400 ms (the adapter's own clock: the core has no timers). Links join the neighbours of the route when an exit of either leads to the other. The cards run along a row in route order and the next row runs back (a serpentine), at the largest scale at which the whole route fits: 8 down to 1 pixel a tile, then 1/2 down to 1/16. The shipped route of two areas is one row at 4 pixels a tile. A name wider than 136 pixels is cut and ends in `..`.
- **Limits.** Links between areas that are not neighbours on the route are not drawn (the spec's route is a chain). A route that fits at no scale is laid out at 1/16 from the top-left, so every area still has a place but it can run off the window. The look (colours, the size of the mark, the blink) is not unit-tested: it is checked by eye in the smoke screenshots.
- **Tests of a pure adapter module.** ADR 0002 lists three thin boundaries as the only exceptions to the core-interface seam. `automap-layout.ts` is Phaser-free, takes plain numbers and returns plain numbers, and `tests/automap-layout.test.ts` tests it directly with hand-worked numbers, as ticket 08 did for `camera.ts` and `area-banner.ts` (the README already lists them). If the seam is to be strict, delete that one file: nothing else depends on it. A short ADR that adds "pure, Phaser-free adapter functions are tested directly" to the exceptions would settle it (proposed, not created).

### How to run it

- `npm.cmd run dev`, then press M at the start: only a disc around the hero is known, the coast is ringed in gold, and the trail is a dark box named UNKNOWN with the link to it already drawn. Esc closes. Hold Right for about 1.2 s and press M again: a stripe of explored terrain runs through the gap in the rock wall; after about 2.5 s the pale patch of the exit on the east edge shows. Cross to the trail (about 3.4 s) and the trail is shown with its name and the hero on it, the coast stays. Let the crabs beat the hero: back at the Checkpoint, everything seen stays.
- `?debug` exposes `window.gameCore`. `npx.cmd vitest run tests/automap.test.ts tests/automap-layout.test.ts` runs the new tests alone; `npm.cmd run check` is the gate.
